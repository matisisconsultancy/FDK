THE HIDDEN PATTERN

AI is becoming an industrial investment cycle.

Compute demand pulls spending into the infrastructure required to make that compute productive.

That creates a broader earnings opportunity extending from silicon into electricity, grids, cooling, networking and automation.

THE AI TRADE IS EXPANDING FROM INTELLIGENCE INTO INDUSTRIAL INFRASTRUCTURE.

THE GREAT SPLIT

The divide is:

PRODUCTIVE CAPACITY vs. INSTALLED CAPACITY.

Both consume CAPEX.

Only one produces utilization, cash flow and returns above the cost of capital.

SPENDING IS NOT THE METRIC. ECONOMIC OUTPUT IS.
