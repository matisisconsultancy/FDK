Why the Return of Jensen Huang Will Not Reverse the Financial Fragmentation of the AI Supercycle
Material inflection confirmed
THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
What happens when financial markets restore their confidence in the leadership of the AI Supercycle, but governments simultaneously restrict the global circulation of the computing power underpinning it?
Two developments on November 23–24 expose this fundamental contradiction.
Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO has reignited investor confidence. Simultaneously, Washington has blacklisted seven Southeast Asian companies, including an Alibaba subsidiary, materially escalating the enforcement of American semiconductor restrictions.

American computing companies could benefit from renewed investor confidence, while Chinese AI companies face increasing uncertainty over access to the infrastructure required to remain competitive.

WHAT'S NEXT — THREE SIGNALS FOR INVESTORS
The computing constraint: Whether restricted offshore capacity materially delays Chinese frontier-model releases.
The substitution premium: Whether Huawei and other domestic accelerator suppliers can achieve competitive performance and economics at industrial scale.
The diffusion advantage: Whether DeepSeek, Alibaba and other Chinese developers can sustain lower cost per completed task despite more expensive or constrained computing access.
No newly verified Chinese frontier-model release has overturned American leadership in today's review. The material change is regulatory and financial.
THE FINAL TAKE
The return of Jensen Huang may restore confidence in the leadership of the AI Supercycle. It does not remove the structural forces fragmenting its technological and financial architecture.
For The Velocity Edge, the governing thesis is strengthened: America leads critical elements of the AI frontier, while China challenges the economics of its deployment.
The next market rotation will increasingly differentiate technological leadership, capital efficiency and geopolitical resilience.
The geography of intelligence is becoming the geography of capital. Investors must price not only the power of AI, but the freedom to deploy it.
