The Rise of AI-Native Cities and the Emergence of a New Geography of Economic Power
Why the global race for intelligence will increasingly be decided by cities — and how the European AI-Native Constellation could redefine the continent's competitive advantage and the future allocation of capital.
THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
What if the next generation of economic superpowers will be determined not by the size of their national economies, but by the capacity of their cities to attract, accumulate, connect and compound intelligence?
Anthropic's reported expansion in Zurich is a signal of a far more consequential transformation: the geographical redistribution of intellectual capital in the AI Supercycle.
Over the past century, Detroit transformed manufacturing, New York transformed finance, Tokyo transformed industrial productivity, Silicon Valley transformed computing and Shenzhen transformed the economics of technological production.
These cities did not simply participate in economic growth. They redefined the mechanisms of wealth creation.
WHY THIS TIME IS DIFFERENT
Artificial intelligence is not simply the outcome of technological innovation. It is increasingly an input into the production of subsequent innovation.
Cities capable of accumulating intelligence can potentially accelerate the production of additional intelligence, generating self-reinforcing cycles of productivity, innovation and capital formation.
Three structural discontinuities distinguish the AI Supercycle from previous economic revolutions:
The unprecedented speed at which intelligence can be deployed across industries and geographical boundaries.
The recursive relationship between intelligence accumulation and innovation.
The ability of relatively small, highly specialised metropolitan ecosystems to exercise global economic influence.
The paradox of the AI Supercycle is that intelligence can become increasingly accessible globally while its frontier creation becomes increasingly concentrated geographically.
The geography of intelligence is becoming a leading indicator of the future geography of wealth.

Exceptional research capabilities, scientific excellence and intellectual capital density. Anthropic's reported expansion reinforces the strategic relevance of smaller, research-intensive metropolitan ecosystems.
Zurich illustrates how the density of intellectual capital can become more consequential than demographic or geographical scale.
MADRID — THE AI CAPITAL MAGNET
The convergence of energy, accelerated computing, infrastructure investment, financial capital and international commercialisation.
Its complementary relationships with Zaragoza and Barcelona could establish an Iberian Intelligence Corridor of global relevance, connecting investment capital, computing infrastructure and scientific research.
Madrid illustrates how infrastructure competitiveness and capital attraction can accelerate metropolitan repositioning.
MILAN — THE INDUSTRIAL INTELLIGENCE MAGNET
The intersection of financial capital, advanced manufacturing, engineering, design and physical AI.

Milan illustrates how established industrial ecosystems can reinvent their competitive advantage through AI deployment at scale.
Zurich creates intelligence. Madrid scales intelligence. Milan industrialises intelligence.
THE CONSTELLATION EFFECT
The second overarching theoretical contribution of our research is the Constellation Effect: the incremental economic value that interconnected metropolitan ecosystems could generate beyond their standalone capabilities.
It operates through eight potentially mutually reinforcing economic mechanisms:
Talent circulation — expanding the effective pool of intellectual capital.
Knowledge recombination — accelerating scientific discovery and innovation.
Capital mobilisation — improving the allocation of financial resources.
Infrastructure scale — increasing computing and energy utilisation.
Industrial productivity — transmitting intelligence across economic sectors.
Market expansion — accelerating international commercialisation.
Economic resilience — diversifying strategic capabilities and dependencies.
Wealth compounding — reinvesting productivity gains into further intelligence accumulation.
The Constellation is the architecture. The Constellation Effect is the economic multiplier.
Europe's historical fragmentation could become a source of differentiated competitive advantage — provided its specialised capabilities are connected at sufficient speed and scale.
The United States has demonstrated the economic power of concentrated innovation ecosystems.
China has demonstrated the economic power of coordinated industrial scaling.
Europe has the opportunity to demonstrate the economic power of interconnected metropolitan intelligence.
THE GREAT REPRICING OF CITIES
The emergence of AI-native cities could trigger a structural geographical reallocation of capital, with increasingly consequential first-, second- and third-order effects.
FIRST-ORDER EFFECTS — THE REALLOCATION OF PRODUCTIVE CAPITAL
Attraction of frontier talent and research capabilities.
Acceleration of AI-related capital expenditure.
Expansion of computing and energy infrastructure.
Increased demand for specialised commercial and residential real estate.
SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS — THE REPRICING OF PRODUCTIVITYIndustrial productivity improvements and operating leverage.
Stronger corporate earnings and capital efficiency.
Higher infrastructure utilisation.
Expansion of entrepreneurial ecosystems and private capital markets.
Increasing economic value of metropolitan connectivity.
THIRD-ORDER EFFECTS — THE REPRICING OF ECONOMIC POWER
Structural changes in corporate valuation multiples.
Geographical rotation of institutional capital.
Differentiation in metropolitan real estate valuations.
Divergence in regional fiscal capacity and creditworthiness.
Reconfiguration of national competitive advantages.
The financial implications extend across industrial equities, energy infrastructure, accelerated computing, high-speed rail, real estate, growth equity and infrastructure debt.
The highest-ranked cities will not necessarily generate the highest investment returns.
The greatest financial opportunities may emerge where accelerating intelligence accumulation and network integration are not yet reflected in asset valuations.

THE INVESTOR CONCLUSION — THE NEXT GEOGRAPHICAL ROTATION OF CAPITAL
The AI Supercycle is not simply creating a new technology investment cycle. It is redefining the geographical distribution of future earnings, productivity growth and returns on invested capital.
For investors, three variables will become increasingly decisive:
The velocity of intelligence accumulation.
The economic multiplier generated by metropolitan connectivity.
The ability to convert technological leadership into sustainable free cash flow.
The emerging opportunity is to identify cities and industrial ecosystems where these forces are accelerating faster than financial markets are repricing their underlying assets.
This requires looking beyond technology equities towards the intersection of energy, computing, industrial automation, high-speed rail, real estate and financial infrastructure.
The European AI-Native Constellation could become a catalyst for a structural rerating of selected European assets — provided that intellectual capital translates into higher productivity, stronger earnings and superior capital efficiency.
The next major market rotation may therefore be not only sectoral but geographical.
The investment imperative is to anticipate where intelligence will compound, where earnings will accelerate and where valuations have yet to reflect that transformation.
THE STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE — FROM INTELLIGENCE TO ECONOMIC POWER
The challenge is no longer simply to attract intelligence, but to convert it into productivity, sustainable earnings, capital appreciation and national prosperity.

Connect universities, financial institutions, industrial companies and infrastructure operators through integrated investment strategies.
Benchmark urban competitiveness against intelligence accumulation velocity, not merely historical economic performance.

KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR EUROPEAN INSTITUTIONS

Connect Europe's fragmented capital markets, energy systems, research ecosystems and industrial capabilities.
Transform metropolitan specialisation into a source of collective competitive advantage.

Make the Constellation Effect a measurable objective of European industrial and economic policy.
WHAT'S NEXT?
The next phase of the AI Supercycle will increasingly reward metropolitan ecosystems capable of accelerating the four grand laws of intelligence accumulation:
SPEED UP — accelerate the transition from scientific discovery to productive deployment.
SCALE UP — expand intelligence capacity through talent, capital and infrastructure.
SCALE OUT — deploy intelligence across industries and metropolitan ecosystems.
SCALE BEYOND — extend economic influence beyond geographical and national boundaries.
Europe's next competitive advantage may lie not in the power of a single star, but in the economic power of its Constellation.
THE FINAL TAKE
Two hundred and fifty years after Adam Smith published The Wealth of Nations, the AI Supercycle is rewriting the economics of prosperity.
In the twentieth century, cities built the industries that created the wealth of nations.
In the twenty-first century, interconnected cities will build the intelligence that determines which nations create wealth.The next great geographical rotation of capital will be shaped by the cities that accumulate intelligence, the constellations that multiply it and the economies that capture its value.
Cities will create intelligence. Constellations will compound wealth. Nations will rise or fall on their ability to connect the two.
