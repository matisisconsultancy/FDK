Trump–Xi lowered geopolitical risk. It did not lower the hurdle rate.

The Washington meeting points toward managed competition, not convergence. The U.S. and China have more room to contain escalation, while the structural contest over AI, semiconductors, critical minerals and industrial capacity continues.

Meanwhile, the U.S. 10-year Treasury touched 5.23%.

That is tonight’s decisive signal:

AI no longer needs to prove that demand exists. It needs to prove that demand can generate returns above the cost of capital.

FIVE SIGNALS

1. Capital is expensive. At 5.23%, distant cash flows lose value. Time-to-cash now matters as much as growth.

2. AI capex is still accelerating. U.S. orders for computers and communications equipment remain exceptionally strong. The Supercycle is becoming physical.

3. Contracts beat forecasts. Anthropic’s $11.6 billion, seven-year Akamai commitment captures the new model:

CONTRACT → CAPACITY → UTILIZATION → CASH FLOW

4. Trump–Xi changes the temperature, not the structure. Washington reduces near-term escalation risk. But two increasingly complete AI-industrial stacks continue to emerge.

5. The market is becoming selective. Capital is not leaving AI. It is moving toward bankable AI.

⸻

THE NEW HIERARCHY

Announced capacity

↓

Contracted capacity

↓

Energized capacity

Cash-generating capacity

At 2% money, markets bought the promise. At 5.2%, they demand proof.

AFTER WASHINGTON

The U.S.–China relationship is moving toward managed bifurcation:

United States: frontier models + accelerated computing + hyperscaler capital.

China: domestic models + semiconductors + memory + power + industrial scale.

The strategic consequence for Europe is unequivocal:

Europe needs a third stack—not a third model.

Industrial AI. Power. Compute. Capital. Critical infrastructure.

⸻

KEY TAKEAWAYS

• Investors: favor contracted demand, secured power and visible cash flow.
• CEOs: measure CAPEX-to-Cash Velocity.
• Europe: build Industrial AI around its physical economy.
• Markets: watch the 10-year Treasury, AI credit spreads and energized capacity.
• The regime: scale alone no longer wins. Bankable scale does.

VELOCITY INSIGHT

The AI Supercycle has reached the underwriting desk.

The next Great Split is:

BANKABLE AI

versus

AI THAT STILL NEEDS TO BE BELIEVED.

At 5.2%, belief is expensive.

Proof is the new premium.
