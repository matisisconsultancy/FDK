Five numbers define the regime.

The U.S. 10-year Treasury touched 5.23%. U.S. computer-related capital-goods orders rose 20.1% year on year. Anthropic committed $11.6 billion to Akamai cloud infrastructure. Brent remains above $100. French sovereign spreads widened beyond 110 basis points over Germany.

They describe the same collision:

**AI DEMAND ↑

→ CAPEX ↑
→ CAPITAL REQUIRED ↑**

while:

**ENERGY RISK ↑

→ YIELDS ↑
→ HURDLE RATE ↑**

AI demand is accelerating inside a more expensive capital regime.

The decisive question is:

WHO CAN TURN AI CAPEX INTO FREE CASH FLOW FAST ENOUGH TO OUTRUN THE HURDLE RATE?

That is today’s Great Split:

Productive AI Capital vs. Capital Consumption.

FIVE SIGNALS

1. EARNINGS — AI STILL OUTRUNS DURATION

AI-linked equities remain resilient despite Treasury yields above 5%.

Implication: retain exposure where earnings revisions and free cash flow support the multiple.

2. CAPEX — AI IS NOW AN INDUSTRIAL CYCLE

Core U.S. capital-goods orders rose 1.6%, with computer-related orders up 20.1% year on year.

Implication: compute, power and networking should now be managed as one industrial capital-allocation system.

3. CONTRACTS — VISIBILITY EARNS THE PREMIUM

Anthropic’s $11.6 billion Akamai commitment validates long-duration AI demand.

Implication: favor capacity backed by secured customers, power and utilization.

4. ENERGY — POWER IS PART OF THE DISCOUNT RATE

Brent remains above $100.

Implication: energy affects AI through both operating economics and financing costs. Secure power is a strategic asset.

5. EUROPE — CAPITAL CONVERSION IS THE BOTTLENECK

European sovereign spreads are widening as strategic investment needs in AI, grids, defense and infrastructure rise.

Implication: Europe must measure time to productive capacity, not announced capex.

THE HIDDEN PATTERN

The regime is Selective Acceleration.

AI demand remains powerful, but capital is becoming less forgiving.

The market is separating:

SCARCITY + CONTRACTED DEMAND + CASH FLOW

from

CAPEX + LEVERAGE + DISTANT MONETIZATION

The next phase will reward companies that convert capital into economic output fastest.

CAPITAL ALLOCATION HIERARCHY

TIER 1 — CORE OVERWEIGHT | OWN THE BOTTLENECK
Accelerated computing, HBM/memory, networking, optics, grid, power and connected capacity. Overweight while scarcity, utilization and pricing power remain intact.

TIER 2 — SELECTIVE OVERWEIGHT | OWN THE CONVERSION
Hyperscalers and AI infrastructure with secure power, contracted demand and strong balance sheets. Add only while incremental FCF keeps pace with incremental AI capex.

TIER 3 — SELECTIVE | OWN MONETIZATION
Agentic AI and applications with distribution, proprietary data and pricing power. Build exposure only when monetization grows faster than compute cost.

TIER 4 — WATCH / UNDERWEIGHT | AVOID CAPITAL TRAPS
Leveraged, long-duration AI dependent on refinancing, uncertain utilization or unresolved power. Stay underweight while economic proof depends on lower rates or future capital raises.

FOR BOARDS OF DIRECTORS

Treat AI as capital allocation—not technology procurement.

Put compute, power and financing on the same dashboard.

Measure time-to-cash-flow, not only deployment speed.

Stress-test higher rates, higher energy costs and lower utilization.

Eliminate single points of failure across cloud, models, power and capital.
