The Velocity Edge
Which industries are building the Next Economy fastest today?
Semiconductors lead because computing demand is broadening across the entire silicon architecture—not only accelerators.
Aerospace and defense move into second because orbital connectivity has become commercial infrastructure while sovereign procurement remains structurally funded.
Cloud remains indispensable, but grid access has become a strategic permission rather than an administrative assumption.
Energy accelerates because electricity—not oil—is now the decisive input into industrial intelligence.
Artificial Intelligence remains the ultimate source of productivity, but capital must now prove that intelligence can become cash flow.
The fastest marginal acceleration is occurring one layer further into the physical economy:
telecommunications;
machinery;
construction;
electrical infrastructure;
and healthcare tools.
Caterpillar provides the defining signal.
The Next Economy is no longer being built fastest where intelligence is merely announced. It is being built where intelligence receives power, receives permission, becomes an industrial order—and earns a return.
