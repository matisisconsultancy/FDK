Friday priced resilience. Monday will test whether it was justified.

The market enters Monday with an unstable mix:

AI leadership. 5%+ yields. $100+ oil.

The key question is no longer whether AI remains the dominant investment theme.

It is whether AI can still command capital when money and energy are both expensive.

That is the next phase of The Great Split.

⸻

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

What breaks first on Monday: oil, bonds or AI leadership?

That answer will tell us far more than another week of headlines.

⸻

THE BIG TAKE

The AI cycle is becoming a contest for control of scarce inputs.

Not just models.

Not just chips.

But:

capital
power
compute
customers
cash flow

The second-order effect is concentration.

Higher rates may not weaken AI.

They may make the AI economy more oligopolistic.

The strongest balance sheets can keep investing.

The weakest will increasingly depend on hyperscalers, sovereign capital or strategic buyers.

THE PRICE OF CAPITAL IS BECOMING A COMPETITIVE MOAT.

⸻

MONDAY MORNING — THREE TESTS

1. OIL

If Brent reverses Friday’s relief, inflation risk immediately returns to the front of the market.

The chain is simple:

OIL ↑ → INFLATION ↑ → YIELDS ↑ → MULTIPLES ↓

The first losers are the most energy-intensive and rate-sensitive businesses.

The first beneficiaries are scarcity assets.

⸻

2. THE U.S. 10-YEAR

Around 5.23% is the first key pressure point.

If yields push decisively higher, Monday becomes a capital-allocation session rather than a growth session.

The market will start asking:

Which businesses genuinely earn above the new hurdle rate?

That favors:

cash flow
pricing power
contracted infrastructure
power
grids
semiconductors

And punishes leverage without visible returns.

⸻

3. AI LEADERSHIP

This is the decisive test.

If AI leaders hold while oil and yields rise, the market is confirming extraordinary pricing power and earnings confidence.

If AI leadership breaks as yields rise, the message changes.

The market would begin questioning not AI itself, but the economics of the AI capex cycle.

That would be Monday’s most consequential signal.

⸻

WHAT COMES NEXT

The market is moving from:

capacity → utilization
narrative → cash flow
cheap capital → strategic financing
technology advantage → system control

That changes where value migrates.

The next leg of the AI trade may increasingly sit upstream:

power
grids
memory
networking
cooling
contracted compute
strategic infrastructure

COMPUTE WITHOUT POWER IS STRANDED CAPITAL.

⸻

EUROPE — THE MONDAY READ

Europe should increasingly be viewed as a portfolio of strategic assets, not one macro trade.

The attractive exposure is where Europe owns scarcity:

grids, rail, automation, semiconductor equipment, mobility and energy infrastructure.

The weak side remains sovereign fragmentation and expensive financing.

BUY THE BOTTLENECKS. NOT THE BETA.

⸻

MONDAY PLAYBOOK

IF OIL ↑ + YIELDS ↑

Favor scarcity, cash flow and infrastructure.

IF OIL ↑ + AI HOLDS

AI leadership strengthens further.

IF YIELDS ↑ + AI BREAKS

Repricing spreads beyond technology.

IF YIELDS EASE + AI HOLDS

Risk appetite broadens.

That is the cleanest map for the Monday open.

⸻

THE WEEK AHEAD

Three questions matter:

Does capital keep concentrating?
If yes, AI consolidation accelerates.

Does power keep repricing?
If yes, energy infrastructure becomes the next technology trade.

Does 5% remain the hurdle rate?
If yes, corporate strategies built on cheap capital must be rewritten.

⸻

KEY TAKEAWAYS

INVESTORS

Watch oil, yields and AI leadership — in that order.

EXECUTIVES

Financing and power are now part of competitive strategy.

POLICYMAKERS

AI sovereignty increasingly depends on energy and capital-market depth.

⸻

THE VELOCITY INSIGHT

The first AI race was about models.

The second was about compute.

The third is about control.

MONDAY WILL TELL US WHETHER THE MARKET HAS STARTED PRICING THAT THIRD RACE.And if it has, The Great Split is about to widen again.
