OPENING THESIS
MEASURED EVIDENCE
TSMC continues to report exceptional revenue momentum tied to advanced-compute demand.
Memory markets remain supported by AI infrastructure requirements.
Large-scale AI deployment is increasingly reaching beyond conventional capex into leases, supplier finance, structured vehicles and long-duration infrastructure commitments.
Frontier-AI companies are simultaneously confronting two financial questions: how revenue should be economically attributed and how increasingly large compute commitments should be financed.
FDK CORE JUDGMENT
The AI cycle is moving from a capacity test to an economics test.
The benchmark now reduces that test to one chain:
Revenue ownership → capital burden → cash conversion → incremental ROIC
The companies that sustain that chain will compound strategic advantage. Those that cannot will see technological velocity outrun financial resilience.

BOARD QUESTIONS
FDK GOVERNANCE JUDGMENT

Capacity → Utilization → Productivity → Revenue → Margin → FCF → ROIC
2. WHAT IS THE TRUE CAPITAL BURDEN?
Include leases, SPVs, supplier finance and other fixed commitments.
3. HOW MUCH REVENUE IS ECONOMICALLY OURS?
Separate recognized, partner-attributed, gross, net and cash economics.
4. WHAT MUST WE OWN — AND WHAT MUST WE CONTROL?
Preserve strategic control without absorbing unnecessary capital.
5. WHERE IS THE NEXT BOTTLENECK?
Test resilience against power, memory, networking, talent, permitting and financing constraints.

SECOND-ORDER MARKET EFFECTS
FDK JUDGMENT
1. CREDIT BECOMES A LEADING INDICATOR
Financing stress may appear before operating demand weakens.
2. SUPPLIER MULTIPLES BECOME MORE DEPENDENT ON CUSTOMER ROIC
Strong unit demand may coexist with valuation pressure if customer economics deteriorate.
3. VALUE MOVES DOWNSTREAM
As infrastructure matures, incremental value should migrate toward software, industrial automation, workflow platforms and vertical AI.
4. DISCLOSURE QUALITY EARNS A PREMIUM
Clearer revenue ownership and infrastructure economics should reduce uncertainty.
5. POWER BECOMES A FINANCIAL VARIABLE
Grid availability increasingly affects utilization, revenue timing and project returns.
6. CAPITAL INTENSITY DRIVES CONSOLIDATION
Expect more partnerships, supplier financing, cloud dependence and M&A.
7. CASH-RICH INCUMBENTS GAIN OPTIONALITY
Self-funded companies gain flexibility as external financing becomes more expensive.
