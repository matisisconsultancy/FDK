The Next Economy is entering a new competitive phase. Corporate leadership is expanding beyond the companies designing and deploying artificial intelligence toward those controlling the infrastructure, technologies and financial mechanisms required to make intelligence economically productive.

The first phase of the AI Supercycle rewarded computing scarcity. The next will reward control of the entire economic-conversion chain — from memory and interconnections to electricity, enterprise deployment and cash generation. The defining question is no longer who leads AI, but who captures the greatest returns as AI becomes an integrated industrial and financial system.

::section The Next Great Market Rotation | from isolated leadership to integrated conversion
Three sources of value are emerging: **Technology Architects** (TSMC, Nvidia, ASML, Broadcom) with scarce capabilities; **Infrastructure Accelerators** (Schneider Electric, Siemens Energy, Corning, Teradyne) in deployment, connectivity and manufacturing; and **Economic Converters** (Microsoft, Amazon, SAP, Palantir) in enterprise monetization and recurring cash. Base case: broadening, more differentiated returns. Upside: accelerating conversion validates investment. Downside: financing-led consolidation as higher capital costs and weak utilization expose interconnected operators.

::takeaways Five Takeaways | investors, executives, policymakers

- Boards — redefine capital productivity around time-to-production, utilization, incremental ROIC and cash conversion.
- Executives — accelerate economic conversion and preserve financial flexibility.
- Policymakers — build competitive industrial ecosystems; Europe's opportunity is scaling industrial strength into AI-enabled productivity.
