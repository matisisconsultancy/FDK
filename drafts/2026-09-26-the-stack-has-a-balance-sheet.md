THE THESIS

THE AI RACE HAS MOVED FROM BUILDING THE STACK TO MAKING THE STACK PAY.

Models proved capability.

Compute proved demand.

The next phase must prove economics.

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

WHO CAN TURN COMPUTE INTO CASH FLOW FAST ENOUGH TO OUTRUN ENERGY AND CAPITAL COSTS?

FIVE SIGNALS

1. CAPITAL — 5% CHANGES THE SCREEN

Fact: The U.S. 10-year closed above 5.15%.

Long-duration AI assets must now justify a materially higher risk premium.

ROIC − WACC IS THE TEST.

2. AGENTS — MONETIZATION MOVES FORWARD

Fact: Microsoft expanded Copilot into coding, always-on agents and deeper Office integration.

The market is shifting from model capability toward paid enterprise productivity.

3. ENERGY — THE ELECTRON ENTERS THE ECONOMICS

Fact: Brent settled at $104.32, with Hormuz uncertainty unresolved.

Energy raises both compute costs and, through inflation and rates, capital costs.

4. EUROPE — AI MEETS THE POWER CONSTRAINT

Fact: Europe wants data-center capacity to rise substantially by 2030, while gas inventories remain well below last year’s level.

Europe’s opportunity sits increasingly in grids, electrical equipment, semiconductor equipment, nuclear and automation.

5. SOVEREIGN CAPITAL — RESILIENCE MOVES UP THE AGENDA

Fact: Qatar has created Doha Investment, a new domestic investment arm within its sovereign-capital architecture.

The broader signal is strategic: sovereign capital is increasingly being directed toward domestic infrastructure, energy security and resilience, rather than portfolio diversification alone.

⸻

THE HIDDEN PATTERN

The common denominator is capital productivity.

Agents must become paid workflows.

Compute must become utilized capacity.

Power must remain affordable.

CAPEX must become cash flow.

THE AI SUPERCYCLE IS MOVING FROM CAPACITY CREATION TO ECONOMIC CONVERSION.

⸻

THE GREAT SPLIT

The divide is no longer AI vs. non-AI.

It is:

PRODUCTIVE AI CAPITAL vs. SPECULATIVE AI CAPITAL.

Productive capital combines real demand, secured power, high utilization, strong balance sheets and returns above the cost of capital.

Speculative capital depends on future adoption, future power, continuous refinancing and lower future rates.

For investors, three questions matter:

Does ROIC clear WACC?

Is demand monetizable?

Does capacity convert into cash?

Everything else is secondary.

THE CLOSE

5% Treasuries did not break the AI Supercycle.

$104 oil keeps energy economics in the valuation.

CAN AI SUSTAINABLY EARN A ROIC ABOVE ITS WACC?

That is the Great Split — between capital that compounds through AI and capital that is merely consumed by AI.
