The AI Supercycle remains intact. The free-capital era does not. At a ~5.2% risk-free rate, scale without returns destroys value. The next re-rating will separate AI cash generators from AI capital consumers.

For investors and boards, the rule is simple: fund intelligence that compounds capital. Cut intelligence that consumes it.
