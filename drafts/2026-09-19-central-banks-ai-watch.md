THE AI SUPERCYCLE HAS REACHED NEUTRAL-RATE DISCOVERY
The AI Supercycle is producing a monetary paradox.
Industrial AI may ultimately be deflationary. But building the Industrial AI economy is proving inflationary first.
The latest signal comes from Japan. The Bank of Japan has raised rates to 1.25%, entering its estimated neutral-rate range just as AI investment, energy scarcity and fiscal spending are increasing global demand for capital.
Japan now joins the Fed, ECB and Bank of England in confronting the same fundamental question:
HAS THE AI SUPERCYCLE RAISED THE NEUTRAL RATE DURING THE BUILD-OUT PHASE?
THE EMERGING CHAIN
AI Expectations→ Capital Formation→ Compute + Energy + Infrastructure Demand→ Competition for Savings→ Higher Long-Term Yields / R*→ Higher Hurdle Rates
Only later comes the countervailing force:
AI Diffusion→ Productivity→ Lower Unit Costs→ Industrial AI Deflation
Central banks are still operating predominantly in the first half of this cycle. That explains an apparently contradictory world: productivity can accelerate while interest rates remain high—or even rise.
THE ECB’S COUNTERWEIGHT
The ECB is introducing an important qualification. Christine Lagarde is resisting a mechanical response to energy inflation: a supply shock should not automatically trigger tighter policy if higher prices simultaneously weaken consumption and growth.
That distinction will matter enormously for AI. A temporary shortage of GPUs, electricity or data-centre capacity is different from AI investment triggering persistent wage, credit and demand inflation.
THE GREAT SPLIT MOVES INTO THE COST OF CAPITAL
For investors, the dividing line becomes increasingly stark:
AI CASH-FLOW GENERATORSvs.AI CAPITAL CONSUMERS
The winners will convert compute and infrastructure into cash flow quickly enough to outrun higher WACC. The vulnerable assets will depend on repeated refinancing, expensive power and long-duration assumptions.
THE EUROPEAN RISK
For Europe, the danger is even greater:
less AI investment + slower productivity diffusion + imported higher global yields = a widening competitiveness gap.
Europe therefore needs to connect:
Savings → Capital Markets → Energy + Grids → AI Infrastructure → Industrial AI → Productivity → Cash Flow → Reinvestment
THE VELOCITY INSIGHT
The monetary equation of the AI Supercycle is becoming:
AI CAPITAL FORMATION → HIGHER R*AI PRODUCTIVITY DIFFUSION → LOWER UNIT COSTS
The decisive variable is time.
If productivity arrives quickly enough, Industrial AI ultimately becomes disinflationary. If capital requirements outrun productivity for too long, the AI Supercycle itself can keep the equilibrium cost of money structurally higher.
AI may eventually make intelligence cheaper. But first, building intelligence is making capital more expensive.
