26 September 2026 | FDK

QUICK TAKE

The monetary thesis is now decisive:

AI is tightening the economy before it is transforming it.

The build-out is already pulling on power, compute, infrastructure and financing. The offset — higher output, lower unit costs and broader efficiency gains — is still arriving.

That means the first-order effect of the AI Supercycle is not disinflation.

It is pressure on capital, inputs and rates.

AI CAPEX
→ DEMAND + INPUT PRESSURE
→ HIGHER COST OF CAPITAL
→ LESS ROOM FOR EASING

Only later:

AI DIFFUSION
→ HIGHER OUTPUT
→ LOWER UNIT COSTS
→ DISINFLATION

The evidence now lines up across three channels.

THE THREE CHANNELS

FED — DEMAND

AI CAPEX
→ COMPUTE + POWER + INFRASTRUCTURE
→ INVESTMENT + FINANCING DEMAND
→ RATE PRESSURE

Williams: the aggregate efficiency effect is not yet clearly visible.

BOE — INFLATION

ENERGY + INPUT COSTS
→ PRICES
→ EXPECTATIONS + WAGES
→ MONETARY RESPONSE

Bailey: policymakers may need to act before second-round effects are fully embedded.

SINGAPORE — SUPPLY RESPONSE

AI ADOPTION
→ SKILLS + JOB REDESIGN
→ HIGHER OUTPUT PER WORKER
→ LOWER UNIT COSTS

Singapore: more than 80,000 financial-sector workers are being retrained to accelerate the transition.

One underlying race:

HOW FAST CAN AI CAPITAL BECOME LOWER-COST OUTPUT?

INVESTOR IMPLICATIONS

Stay selective on duration while capital demand runs ahead of measurable gains.

Own the build-out selectively: compute, power, networks, data centers, cooling and semiconductor equipment.

Favor businesses already converting AI into margins, cash flow and higher returns on capital.

Watch the inflection in:

OUTPUT PER HOUR
UNIT COSTS
MARGINS
LABOR UTILIZATION
RETURNS ON AI SPENDING

When those metrics turn decisively, markets can begin pricing AI as an economic transformation cycle rather than primarily a capital cycle.

EUROPE’S TEST

Europe must compress one chain:

CAPITAL → COMPUTE → ADOPTION → OUTPUT

The risk is not simply investing too little.

It is investing heavily while converting too slowly.

Europe needs fewer years between AI CapEx and measurable economic return.

THE VELOCITY INSIGHT

The next monetary regime will be shaped by one variable:

HOW QUICKLY AI MOVES FROM RAISING THE COST OF CAPITAL TO LOWERING THE COST OF PRODUCTION.

THE INVESTOR VERDICT

OWN THE ENABLERS SELECTIVELY.
DEMAND CASH FLOW.
RESPECT RATE RISK.
ROTATE WHEN OUTPUT GAINS BECOME DATA.
