30 September 2026 | FDK

THE BIG TAKE

Three signals now line up:

• The Fed is increasingly focused on higher real yields and the financing intensity of the technology investment cycle.
• The ECB sees AI pressure across semiconductor capacity, investment and corporate financing.
• Financial supervisors are increasingly examining concentration, leverage and interconnectedness around the AI ecosystem.

The implication:

AI IS ALREADY RESHAPING FINANCIAL CONDITIONS BEFORE ITS FULL PRODUCTIVITY DIVIDEND IS VISIBLE.

Two second-order effects now matter most.

1. CREDIT → THE BUILD-OUT BECOMES THE FAULT LINE

The AI Supercycle is increasingly financed through a much broader ecosystem:

DATA CENTRES
POWER
GRIDS
PROJECT FINANCE
PRIVATE CREDIT
LEVERAGED SUPPLIERS

The key question is no longer simply whether AI demand continues.

It is:

CAN THE CASH FLOWS CLEAR THE NEW COST OF CAPITAL?

If expected returns weaken while real yields remain high:

REFINANCING PRESSURE
→ WIDER SPREADS
→ CAPEX CUTS
→ PROJECT CANCELLATIONS
→ ASSET REPRICING

The next AI correction could therefore move from equities into credit — and from credit into real investment.

That is the hidden transmission channel investors should watch.

1. EQUITIES → FROM AI BUILD-OUT TO AI ECONOMIC CAPTURE

The first phase rewarded those selling the infrastructure.

The next phase should increasingly reward those using AI to transform their economics.

The hierarchy shifts:

AI NARRATIVE
→ AI CAPEX
→ AI ADOPTION
→ PRODUCTIVITY
→ MARGIN EXPANSION
→ FREE CASH FLOW

Higher discount rates accelerate that transition because distant promises become less valuable.

The next leadership group should increasingly be companies already demonstrating:

LOWER UNIT COSTS
HIGHER OUTPUT PER WORKER
MARGIN EXPANSION
HIGHER RETURNS ON CAPITAL
VISIBLE FREE CASH FLOW

The new divide is no longer:

AI vs NON-AI.

It is:

AI CAPITAL CONSUMERS
vs
AI ECONOMIC CAPTORS.

INVESTOR VERDICT

WATCH CREDIT FIRST.
FOLLOW CASH FLOW SECOND.

Stress-test refinancing beneath the AI build-out.

Then shift equity exposure toward businesses proving that AI raises margins and returns rather than merely CapEx.

THE NEXT MARKET ROTATION BEGINS WHEN CAPITAL STOPS REWARDING WHO BUILDS THE MOST AI — AND STARTS REWARDING WHO EXTRACTS THE MOST ECONOMIC VALUE FROM IT.
