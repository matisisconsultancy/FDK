America Has a System. China Has a System. Europe Has a Deadline.

The Trump–Xi summit clarifies the next phase of the AI race.

The competition is no longer principally about models, chips or Gigafactories. It is about who can turn capital and energy into intelligence—and intelligence into productivity, cash flow and greater technological power.

The United States has a system. China is building another.

Europe has the components. It does not yet have the system.

CAPITAL → POWER → COMPUTE → INDUSTRIAL AI → PRODUCTIVITY → CASH FLOW → CAPITAL

FROM MODELS TO SYSTEMS

The AI race has evolved:

MODELS → COMPUTE → INFRASTRUCTURE → SYSTEMS

A more managed U.S.–China rivalry could actually accelerate investment on both sides:

Risk ↓ → AI CAPEX ↑ → Scale ↑ → Ecosystem Depth ↑

Europe is therefore not competing against today’s gap.

It is competing against the speed at which that gap compounds.

FROM GIGAFACTORIES TO COMPOUNDING MW

A Gigafactory creates capacity.

The real objective is to create an economic flywheel:

POWER → COMPUTE → INDUSTRIAL AI → PRODUCTIVITY → CASH FLOW → REINVESTMENT

Call it Compounding MW: AI capacity that generates the demand and capital required to finance its own expansion.

That is the difference between hosting AI infrastructure and owning the economics of AI.

Location is not sovereignty. Value capture is.

EUROPE’S EDGE

Europe should not replicate America or China.

Its advantage is the combination of:

capital + low-carbon power + grids + telecoms + engineering + manufacturing + robotics + mobility.

The opportunity is Industrial AI at continental scale.

Gigafactories are not the destination.

They are the infrastructure of the flywheel.

THE VELOCITY INSIGHT

Europe’s question is no longer:

Can we build enough Gigafactories?

It is:

Can Europe build a system where capital creates intelligence, intelligence creates productivity, and productivity finances more intelligence?

That is how compute becomes economic power.

INVESTORS — TAKEAWAYS

• Move beyond MW: focus on cash flow per AI MW.
• Prioritize Compounding MW: power + compute + customers + applications.
• Follow the bottlenecks: grids, storage, cooling, photonics and fiber.
• Watch telecoms: distribution can turn compute into contracted demand.

EU POLICYMAKERS — TAKEAWAYS

• Measure European value captured per MW, not MW alone.
• Connect Gigafactories directly to Industrial AI demand.
• Use public procurement to crowd in private capital.
• Integrate AI + energy + semiconductors + telecoms + capital markets into one strategy.
• Build a continental system, not 27 national ones.

The endpoint of the AI race is not the model, the GPU or the Gigafactory.

It is the system that turns intelligence into capital—and capital into more intelligence.

America has one. China is building another. Europe has a deadline.
