THE VELOCITY EDGE — CENTRAL BANKS & AI WATCH

AI IS CHANGING THE PRICE OF CAPITAL BEFORE IT CHANGES THE PRICE OF EVERYTHING ELSE

29 September 2026 | FDK

MONETARY THESIS

AI IS ALREADY A TIGHTENING SHOCK THROUGH CAPITAL FORMATION — WHILE THE DISINFLATIONARY PAYOFF FROM PRODUCTIVITY REMAINS DELAYED.

Three signals now point in the same direction:

REAL YIELDS — Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack points to heavy technology investment intensifying financing demand.

CAPACITY — the ECB sees AI pressure in semiconductor supply, investment and corporate financing.

FINANCIAL STABILITY — Kansas City Fed President Jeff Schmid is asking whether the AI ecosystem itself could become systemically important.

Together, they describe one monetary sequence:

AI CAPEX
→ INPUT + FINANCING PRESSURE
→ TIGHTER FINANCIAL CONDITIONS
→ LESS ROOM TO CUT

Only later:

AI DIFFUSION
→ LOWER UNIT COSTS
→ DISINFLATION
→ MORE ROOM TO EASE

The asymmetry is decisive:

THE TIGHTENING EFFECT IS HERE.
THE EASING EFFECT IS STILL FORMING.

CENTRAL-BANK IMPLICATION

AI does not need to trigger another rate hike to matter.

If the build-out keeps capital demand, real yields and input costs elevated, central banks may have to:

CUT LATER
CUT LESS
OR TIGHTEN AGAIN IF SECOND-ROUND INFLATION BROADENS.

The key variable is the lag between capital formation and productivity diffusion.

TWO SECOND-ORDER EFFECTS

1. CAPITAL CONCENTRATES

HIGHER HURDLE RATES
→ WEAKER FIRMS RETRENCH
→ CAPITAL FLOWS TO LEADERS
→ CAPABILITY GAPS WIDEN

This is how monetary tightening can accelerate the K-shaped economy.

1. THE BUILD-OUT BECOMES A CREDIT CYCLE

HIGH REAL YIELDS + WEAKER AI RETURNS
→ REFINANCING PRESSURE
→ WIDER SPREADS
→ CAPEX CUTS + PROJECT FAILURES

This is how an AI valuation correction can become a credit and investment event.

INVESTOR TAKEAWAY

The next phase is not about owning “more AI.”

It is about identifying:

WHO CAN ACCESS CAPITAL
WHO CAN CLEAR THE HURDLE RATE
WHO CAN SELF-FINANCE
WHO SITS ON FRAGILE LEVERAGE

Favor scarce infrastructure selectively.

Demand strong balance sheets and cash-flow conversion.

Track refinancing risk underneath the build-out.

THE REGIME FLIPS WHEN AI STOPS TIGHTENING THROUGH CAPITAL FORMATION — AND STARTS EASING THROUGH LOWER UNIT COSTS.
