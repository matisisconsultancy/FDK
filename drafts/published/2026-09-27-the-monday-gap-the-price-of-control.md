Friday priced resilience. Monday will test whether it was justified.

The market enters Monday with an unstable mix: AI leadership, 5%+ yields, $100+ oil. The key question is no longer whether AI remains the dominant investment theme. It is whether AI can still command capital when money and energy are both expensive.

That is the next phase of *The Great Split*.

::stats
5.23% :: U.S. 10-year — the first key pressure point
$100+ :: Oil per barrel weighing on inflation risk
5%+ :: The prevailing hurdle rate for capital

::section The Fundamental Question | What breaks first
What breaks first on Monday: oil, bonds or AI leadership? That answer will tell us far more than another week of headlines.

::section The Big Take | A contest for scarce inputs
The AI cycle is becoming a contest for control of scarce inputs. Not just models. Not just chips. But capital, power, compute, customers and cash flow.

The second-order effect is concentration. Higher rates may not weaken AI — they may make the AI economy more oligopolistic. The strongest balance sheets can keep investing. The weakest will increasingly depend on hyperscalers, sovereign capital or strategic buyers.

::pull The price of capital
The price of capital is becoming a **competitive moat**.

::signals Monday Morning — Three Tests | The order that matters
# Oil
If Brent reverses Friday's relief, inflation risk immediately returns to the front of the market. The chain is simple: oil up → inflation up → yields up → multiples down. The first losers are the most energy-intensive and rate-sensitive businesses; the first beneficiaries are ==scarcity assets==.
signal: Watch Brent for the fastest route back to inflation risk.

# The U.S. 10-Year
Around 5.23% is the first key pressure point. If yields push decisively higher, Monday becomes a capital-allocation session rather than a growth session. The market will start asking which businesses genuinely earn above the new hurdle rate — favoring cash flow, pricing power, contracted infrastructure, power, grids and semiconductors, and punishing leverage without visible returns.
signal: Above 5.23%, allocation replaces growth as the story.

# AI Leadership
This is the decisive test. If AI leaders hold while oil and yields rise, the market is confirming extraordinary pricing power and earnings confidence. If AI leadership breaks as yields rise, the market would begin questioning not AI itself, but the economics of the AI capex cycle.
signal: The most consequential signal of the Monday open.

::section What Comes Next | Where value migrates
The market is moving from capacity to utilization, from narrative to cash flow, from cheap capital to strategic financing, and from technology advantage to system control. That changes where value migrates.

The next leg of the AI trade may increasingly sit upstream: power, grids, memory, networking, cooling, contracted compute and strategic infrastructure.

::pull Stranded capital
Compute without power is **stranded capital**.

::section Europe — The Monday Read | Own the scarcity
Europe should increasingly be viewed as a portfolio of strategic assets, not one macro trade. The attractive exposure is where Europe owns scarcity: grids, rail, automation, semiconductor equipment, mobility and energy infrastructure. The weak side remains sovereign fragmentation and expensive financing.

::pull Bottlenecks, not beta
Buy the ==bottlenecks==. Not the beta.

::patterns Monday Playbook | The cleanest map for the open
# If Oil ↑ + Yields ↑
Favor scarcity, cash flow and infrastructure.

# If Oil ↑ + AI Holds
AI leadership strengthens further.

# If Yields ↑ + AI Breaks
Repricing spreads beyond technology.

# If Yields Ease + AI Holds
Risk appetite broadens.

::section The Week Ahead | Three questions that matter
Does capital keep concentrating? If yes, AI consolidation accelerates.

Does power keep repricing? If yes, energy infrastructure becomes the next technology trade.

Does 5% remain the hurdle rate? If yes, corporate strategies built on cheap capital must be rewritten.

::takeaways Key Takeaways | For each seat at the table
- **Investors:** Watch oil, yields and AI leadership — in that order.
- **Executives:** Financing and power are now part of competitive strategy.
- **Policymakers:** AI sovereignty increasingly depends on energy and capital-market depth.

::section The Velocity Insight | The third race
The first AI race was about models. The second was about compute. The third is about control. Monday will tell us whether the market has started pricing that third race.

::close And if it has, ==The Great Split== is about to widen again.
