Kashkari has raised his estimate of the neutral funds rate to **3.25%**, with the AI investment boom one reason equilibrium rates may be higher.

That is the macro signal: AI is not just raising growth expectations. It is raising investment demand faster than productive capacity can respond.

::stats
3.25% :: Kashkari's revised neutral funds rate estimate
First :: Capital demand moves before supply capacity
Later :: AI diffusion delivers productivity and disinflation
2 :: Variables that decide the next rotation — real rates and cash-flow conversion

The transmission runs in sequence: AI capex drives stronger demand for savings and financing, which lifts the equilibrium real rate, raises market real yields, pushes up hurdle rates, and forces more selective capital allocation.

Only later does the offset arrive. AI diffusion raises productivity, expands supply capacity, lowers unit costs, and delivers disinflation.

::pull THE ASYMMETRY
The macro asymmetry is decisive: capital demand moves first, and supply capacity catches up later.

::signals What Higher Rates Decide | the two transmission channels

# Rates → Capital Selectivity
Higher real yields determine who can keep investing. Cash-rich AI leaders can continue funding the build-out.

Weaker firms face higher financing costs, which force lower capex, slower adoption, and wider competitive gaps.
signal: Real yields sort the field into those who can keep building and those who cannot.

# Equities → From AI Exposure to AI Economic Capture
Higher hurdle rates impose a harder market test. Distant AI promises meet higher discounting and lower valuation support, while visible AI returns deliver higher margins, higher ROIC, free cash flow, and self-funded reinvestment.

The hierarchy is shifting from AI narrative to AI capex to AI adoption to AI monetization to free cash flow. The new divide: **AI monetizers vs AI capital consumers.**
signal: The market now prices economic capture, not AI exposure.

::section The Recursive Loop | where the regime meets the K-shaped economy
A strong balance sheet enables more AI investment, which builds greater capability, which generates higher cash flow, which funds more reinvestment.

That is where the monetary regime meets the K-shaped economy — and where the ==Law of Recursive Capital Accumulation== starts to compound.

::takeaways Investor verdict | watch two variables
- **Real rates** — the cost of staying in the build-out.
- **Cash-flow conversion** — the proof of economic capture.
- The next equity rotation will favor the companies that can keep investing.

::close The winners will ==turn AI into cash flow faster than their competitors==.
