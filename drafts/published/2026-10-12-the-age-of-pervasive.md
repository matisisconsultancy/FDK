The next technological revolution will not be defined by the devices we use. It will be defined by the intelligence embedded in everything around us — and by the competing economic systems seeking to control it.

On October 23, Apple begins selling its first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo. On October 13, Meta expands its Ray-Ban Display AI glasses into France, Italy and Germany. These are not merely two product launches. Apple is making the personal device more capable; Meta is attempting to make the device less central. Yet the larger revolution lies beyond both.

We are entering the **Age of Pervasive Intelligence** — in which intelligence is no longer confined to a phone, app or screen, but becomes a distributed capability embedded in devices, vehicles, factories, infrastructure and cities. **Agentic AI** provides the decisive acceleration: artificial intelligence moves from answering questions to executing authorized tasks, coordinating services and optimizing interconnected systems.

::pull FROM DEVICES TO DECISIONS
The next Great Split will separate the companies and economies that control the architecture of intelligence from those that merely consume its output.

::section The New Paradigm | From personal to pervasive computing
Three regimes have defined modern computing: centralized mainframes, personal and mobile computing, and now **AI-Native Pervasive Computing** — distributed intelligence, continuous context, agentic software and interoperable devices.

The relationship inverts. In the mobile era, consumers learned to operate devices and apps. In the pervasive era, technology learns to interpret human objectives. Instead of selecting an application and executing a task, the user expresses an intention; an authorized system interprets the objective, identifies relevant services, coordinates execution and presents an outcome.

This relocates economic value. The application becomes less visible; the intelligent orchestrator becomes decisive. The defining transition is **from device-centric computing to intelligence-centric economic systems**.

::section Apple vs. Meta | The intelligent device versus the intelligent environment
Apple's iPhone Duo embodies *consolidation*: portability plus a larger working surface, advanced computation and an integrated ecosystem, consolidating more digital activity — and services revenue — inside Apple's controlled environment.

Meta follows the opposite trajectory: making intelligence accessible during ordinary physical activity, without retrieving a phone. Its Neural Band adds another interface, interpreting subtle muscular signals. **Apple wants to own the intelligent device; Meta wants to own the intelligent moment.**

The two may coexist — *ambient intelligence* for immediate assistance, *immersive intelligence* for complex work. The strategic contest concerns the transition between them: Who owns identity? Who remembers context? Who determines which agent receives an instruction, authorizes transactions, and captures the value when the task completes?

::section The Agentic Acceleration | From intelligence that responds to intelligence that executes
Conventional AI generates information. Agentic AI plans sequences, calls specialized tools, coordinates services and executes tasks within defined permissions. The opportunity is not faster information — it is the **reduction of the coordination costs** required to turn information into action.

Adoption is accelerating faster than measured returns: 40% of large enterprises are scaling agents, yet only 37% report positive EBIT impact and just 6% qualify as high performers. Gartner forecasts 80% of 2028 agentic returns from domain-specific agents — the next acceleration depends not on larger general models but on specialized intelligence deployed across real processes.

A self-reinforcing loop emerges: interface friction declines, the value of each interaction rises as agents execute multistep workflows, specialized agents extend intelligence into the physical economy, and successful deployment funds further diffusion. But the flywheel is not automatic — security failures, expensive inference and unreliable execution can interrupt it. The decisive metric becomes **cost per successfully completed economic task**.

The productivity discontinuity occurs when AI stops improving individual tasks and begins redesigning the system connecting them — enabling smaller teams to oversee more extensive intelligent systems, reducing working capital, raising asset utilization and improving return on invested capital. But propagating agent errors and poorly defined permissions create new operational and financial exposure. Winning organizations combine agentic capability with rigorous oversight.

::section The Great Intelligence Divide | Two global architectures compete
The Apple–Meta clash is the consumer face of a far larger struggle. Two strategic architectures are emerging.

**Platform-Centric Intelligence** — the U.S. ecosystem (Nvidia, Apple, Meta, Microsoft, Google, Amazon) excels at frontier models, operating systems, cloud, enterprise software and global platforms, concentrating value around high-performance computing and attracting extraordinary capital.

**Industrially Embedded Intelligence** — China's manufacturing scale, electronics supply chains, EV and robotics sectors, plus advancing open-weight models, favor distributing intelligence throughout physical products and industrial processes.

::patterns Comparative Strategic Strengths | Relative advantage, not classification
# U.S.-Centered Ecosystem
Strengths: frontier models, platforms, cloud, advanced chips. Deployment edge in high-value software and global services. Deep equity and credit markets. Constraints: energy, capital intensity, infrastructure delivery. Value capture: platform rents and high-margin services.
# China-Centered Ecosystem
Strengths: manufacturing, devices, robotics, supply chains. Deployment edge in industrial integration and hardware scale. Industrial finance and state support. Constraints: advanced-chip access, trade restrictions. Value capture: industrial productivity and integrated products.

The next divide is less about which country builds the most sophisticated model than which system combines **intelligence, physical deployment and sustainable capital returns** most effectively. The likely outcome is not two closed blocs but *asymmetric interdependence* — India pursuing sovereignty while buying U.S. computing; Gulf economies investing in U.S.-aligned infrastructure while trading with Asian manufacturers; a network of overlapping dependencies. The next balance of power depends not only on who invents intelligence, but on who controls its deployment.

::section Europe's Third Architecture | Trusted industrial intelligence
Duplicating every layer of the American or Chinese stacks would require enormous capital. Europe's strengths lie elsewhere: industrial engineering, precision manufacturing, transportation, electrical equipment, aerospace, automotive and regulated infrastructure — extensive installed capital, a natural environment for specialized Agentic AI.

Europe can pursue a third architecture: **Trusted Industrial Intelligence** — embedding secure, interoperable, financially productive AI across factories, railways, energy networks, ports and public services. Yet it faces fragmented capital markets, uneven digital adoption, expensive energy, complex permitting and weak research-to-deployment links. America can lead in platform intelligence, China in industrial diffusion; Europe must compete to lead in trusted industrial intelligence — measurable productivity, not expenditure for its own sake.

::section The Execution Economy | From apps to agents, attention to results
The smartphone era organized an economy around applications, app stores and attention. Agentic AI challenges that: an intelligent orchestrator becomes the principal point through which consumers access services, leaving the underlying apps necessary but less visible.

Three consequences follow. Distribution power moves toward orchestration — the next gatekeeper controls how services are recommended and executed. Software development becomes agent-assisted: 32% of organizations say they declined to purchase a software product or feature because they could build it internally with agentic coding tools, pressuring undifferentiated vendors. And monetization shifts toward completed outcomes — transaction fees, enterprise licensing and charges linked to verified execution.

::pull THE ECONOMY OF RESULTS
The Attention Economy monetized engagement. The emerging ==Execution Economy== will monetize results.

::section The New Capital Formation Cycle | From accelerated computing to accelerated returns
The first phase of the AI Supercycle has been dominated by infrastructure — data centers, advanced chips, accelerated computing, energy, networking. Returns remain uneven. The second phase deploys intelligence across consumer activity, enterprise workflows and physical infrastructure.

Investors must distinguish the capital required to *produce* intelligence from the value generated when it is *deployed*. A railway's AI only creates value when it improves traffic management, cuts maintenance, raises availability and optimizes energy. The key distinction is between companies dependent on continuous new investment and those generating progressively greater output from installed capital. If agents reduce coordination costs and improve asset utilization, they raise the productivity of existing capital — helping bridge the gap between the buildout's capital requirements and the need for sustainable returns. That process is not automatic: models must be reliable, execution secure, savings measurable, and total operating costs below the value generated. The next phase will be less forgiving — capital markets will reward the conversion of intelligence into productive cash flow, not computing capacity alone.

::section Three Scenarios for 2030 | Analytical possibilities, not forecasts
**Integrated Intelligence Platform** — a few large firms retain control over operating systems, orchestration, identity and distribution; platform economics remain powerful; the risk is concentration of power.

**Fragmented Intelligence World** — the U.S. and China develop separate architectures; incompatible standards raise deployment costs; investment follows geopolitical alignment; efficiency suffers.

**Pervasive Intelligence Economy** — intelligence embeds across devices, industry and infrastructure via interoperable, permissioned services; enterprises reorganize around intelligent execution; the largest returns arise from productive deployment across capital assets. This offers the greatest potential for broad productivity — and requires the most demanding combination of interoperability, financial discipline and institutional trust.

::takeaways Five Strategic Takeaways | For investors, boards and policymakers
- **Redefine the investment thesis** — the frontier is no longer personal computing but Pervasive Intelligence across consumer behavior, enterprise operations and physical infrastructure.
- **Recognize Agentic AI as the acceleration mechanism** — measure completed tasks, cost efficiency, cash conversion and incremental ROIC, not generated information.
- **Position for the next advantage** — control over models alone is insufficient; identity, trusted orchestration, semiconductors, interoperable services, energy and industrial deployment will determine value.
- **Prepare for a more complex U.S.–China divide** — manage technological dependencies without assuming the world splits into two closed blocs.
- **Make Industrial AI Europe's renewal** — combine technological sovereignty with established strengths in manufacturing, mobility and infrastructure; pursue measurable productivity.

::close The first computing revolution placed intelligence inside institutions; the second placed it in individuals' hands; the third is embedding it throughout the physical economy. The future will not belong to whoever builds the most advanced model or sells the most sophisticated device — it will belong to those who can ==transform intelligence into lasting economic advantage==.
