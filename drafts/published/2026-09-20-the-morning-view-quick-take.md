The race has moved through five stages — best model, best compute, best infrastructure, best capital, and now best *terms of access*. That final step is becoming decisive.

The fundamental question is no longer who owns the best technology. It is who sets the terms under which intelligence is built, financed, powered and deployed.

::pull THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
Who sets the terms of access to the full AI stack?

::signals Five Signals | the convergence taking shape
# AI is now part of U.S.–China economic diplomacy
Scott Bessent and He Lifeng meet today ahead of the Trump–Xi summit on 24 September, with AI, rare earths, tariffs and critical minerals on the agenda. The contest is shifting from pure technological decoupling toward negotiated technological interdependence.
signal: Decoupling is giving way to negotiated interdependence.

# China and the West are running different capital regimes
The Fed, ECB and BOJ have tightened; China has kept benchmark lending rates unchanged. The contrast is becoming structural — market-driven capital formation versus state-directed industrial capital formation. Different systems, same objective: control the physical stack of AI.
signal: Two capital regimes, one target — the physical stack.

# Europe's AI opportunity is real — but conditional
The IMF estimates AI could add roughly 1% to European productivity over five years, while around 60% of workers in advanced European economies are highly exposed to AI. Europe's problem is not just technology — it is energy, capital, labor, single market and infrastructure. Without integration, Europe risks adopting AI while exporting much of the economic rent.
signal: Without integration, Europe adopts AI but exports the rent.

# AI is moving into the physical world
Anthropic has established a physical biology laboratory and is exploring how Claude can interact with automated experimental systems under human oversight. The progression accelerates: text → code → agents → robotics → physical experiments → new knowledge. This is the beginning of Industrial AI.
signal: The stack is crossing from text into the physical world.

# Energy security is becoming defense policy
The Middle East continues to show that energy security is no longer simply about production. It is production, routes, pipelines, ports, storage and military protection. The barrel is fungible; the infrastructure delivering it is not.
signal: The barrel is fungible — the infrastructure is not.

::section The Investor Takeaway | where the scarcity rents move
The strongest scarcity rents are moving away from generic AI exposure and toward the assets AI cannot function without: compute, memory, optics, power, grid infrastructure, critical minerals, permitted capacity and physical AI.

The question is no longer who benefits from AI. It is ==who owns the bottleneck — and can change the terms?==
