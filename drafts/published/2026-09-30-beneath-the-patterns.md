The AI Supercycle has crossed a financial threshold. With long-term rates above 5%, capital committed today must overcome a materially higher hurdle while AI assets face rapid depreciation and technological obsolescence.

At the same time, the stack is diverging. Model capability is proliferating, but packaging, power, cooling and grid access remain constrained. The result is a redistribution of pricing power toward the physical layers and a higher financial burden on companies funding capacity ahead of cash generation.

The emerging divide is **Productive AI Capital vs. Trapped AI Capital**. The fundamental question beneath the GVI is now clear: which corporations can translate structural AI leadership into durable cash returns before financing cost, depreciation and obsolescence absorb the advantage?

::signals The New Laws | The economics beneath the cycle
# Capacity Has to Earn
The AI cycle has created two financial paths. Compounding capital moves Capex → Revenue → FCF → ROIC → Reinvestment. Stranded capital runs Capex → Capacity → Depreciation → Financing Cost → Margin Pressure. The difference is not technological ambition — it is the distance between capital committed and cash generated.
signal: That distance is now one of the most important corporate variables in the AI economy.

# Physical Scarcity Determines Where Pricing Power Forms
The physical stack — packaging, memory, networking, power, cooling, grid access — cannot expand at software speed. As intelligence becomes easier to reproduce, these harder-to-replicate layers gain relative strategic value. TSMC, Schneider Electric, Siemens Energy and Vertiv increasingly sit at this intersection.
signal: The first-order AI trade was intelligence; the second-order trade is scarcity.

# Time-to-Power Is a Financial Variable
Power determines when infrastructure can begin producing revenue. Every delay adds financing cost, depreciation, obsolescence and deferred revenue — affecting project economics directly.
signal: Time-to-power should be treated alongside capex, backlog and capacity as a core financial KPI.

# Backlog Will Be Repriced
The old assumption held that more backlog meant more visibility. The emerging hierarchy is Announced → Contracted → Financed → Powered → Cash-Generating. The farther backlog moves through the chain, the higher its economic quality.
signal: Nominal backlog measures demand; economic backlog measures monetizability.

::section The Evolutionary Financial Dynamics | Five stages of the Supercycle
The AI Supercycle is moving through five stages: **I — Build** (models, chips, data centers); **II — Bottleneck** (power, packaging, cooling, grid); **III — Deploy** (capacity reaches customers); **IV — Convert** (revenue becomes cash); and **V — Reprice** (markets separate structural Velocity from economic capture).

The next rotation therefore occurs inside AI: Models → Bottlenecks → Distribution → Security → Cash.

::section Board-Level Implications | Five questions for the next dollar
Boards should ask five questions: Is AI capital earning above its cost? How quickly is investment becoming free cash flow? Where is the binding physical bottleneck? Is time-to-cash shortening? Is incremental ROIC improving?

The governing question is singular: does the next dollar of AI investment increase future cash generation faster than it increases the capital base?

::takeaways Five Takeaways | The shape of the next divide
- The AI cycle has moved from capital formation to return formation.
- Physical scarcity is concentrating pricing power in the layers hardest to reproduce.
- Higher-for-longer increases the cost of every delay between investment and cash generation.
- Backlog will increasingly be valued by economic quality, not nominal size.
- The next AI rotation occurs inside the stack: from technological creation toward bottlenecks, distribution, security and cash.

::close Technology creates strategic position. Capital productivity determines whether that position compounds — the next AI divide is not builders versus laggards, it is ==compounding capital versus stranded capital==.
