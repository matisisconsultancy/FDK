::section Recovered development | Poste Italiane
Important earlier development recovered during today's research: On November 12, Poste Italiane disclosed a co-investment fund targeting €600 million, with an initial 20 MW data-center project involving TIM, Noovle and outside investors. The proposed financing model uses project equity alongside substantial off-balance-sheet debt.

Crucially, €600 million is the fund's overall fundraising target, not a confirmed allocation to data centers or an awarded EuroHPC Gigafactory. The initiative is strategically relevant because it offers a possible mechanism for mobilizing Italian institutional and insurance savings into digital infrastructure.

Its November 12 announcement predates the previous review, so it is recorded here as a recovered development rather than presented as new November 15 news.

::section What still counts | the decisive indicators
Other newly published items, including a British early-stage investment in GPU waste-heat reuse, do not yet constitute material Gigafactory-scale capital formation. The next decisive indicators remain binding project financing, secured grid capacity, verified tender submissions, and construction or commissioning milestones.
