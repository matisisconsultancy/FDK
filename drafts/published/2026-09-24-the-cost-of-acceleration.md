This morning opens with a five-number collision: **5.11%**. **9.75%**. **$11.1 billion**. **$10 billion-plus**. **2.8 million downloads in two weeks**.

Those five numbers frame the market. Put differently: AI demand is accelerating, but the cost of financing that acceleration is accelerating with it. The fundamental question is now unavoidable — can AI returns rise faster than the cost of the capital, energy and infrastructure required to produce them?

The next Great Split is increasingly between AI systems that convert scale into cash flow and those that consume capital faster than they monetize intelligence.

::signals Five Signals | the forces reshaping the cycle
# Capital — The 5% World
The U.S. 10-year Treasury is above 5%, while Japanese yields have reached multi-decade highs. Expensive money is becoming a structural constraint, and capital productivity is becoming as important as technological leadership.
signal: Expensive money is now a structural constraint.

# Leverage — The 9.75% Coupon
SoftBank raised **$11.1 billion** in bonds, with dollar coupons as high as **9.75%**, largely to finance its OpenAI exposure. Frontier AI has moved decisively into public credit markets — and the technology must generate returns fast enough to outrun the coupon.
signal: Frontier AI now lives in public credit markets.

# Demand — The Agentic Inflection
Meta's Muse reached **2.8 million downloads in two weeks**, while Charm pushes AI toward an always-on interface. The interaction model is shifting from chat to delegation. The next demand metric is not users — it is *tasks × duration × agents*.
signal: The model shifts from chat to delegation.

# Infrastructure — The Gulf Full Stack
Microsoft plans to invest more than **$10 billion** in Gulf cloud and AI infrastructure through 2030, alongside major connectivity investment. AI infrastructure is clustering where energy, capital, compute, connectivity and resilience can be assembled together. The winning geography may be the one capable of supplying the complete system.
signal: Winners will supply the complete system.

# Geopolitics — Managed Interdependence
The Trump–Xi meeting takes place today after Washington and Beijing extended their trade truce and explored an AI incident-notification mechanism. The emerging framework is neither full decoupling nor détente — *compete, contain, communicate, continue trading*. Predictable boundaries around competition may become more valuable than temporary tariff concessions.
signal: Predictable boundaries beat temporary concessions.

::section The Collision Of Two Curves | return on intelligence vs. cost of acceleration
The deeper pattern is the collision between two curves. **Technology Velocity**: models, agents, inference, usage. **Capital Intensity**: debt, power, data centers, networking, resilience. Return on intelligence versus the cost of acceleration.

Europe faces the hardest version of this equation. It needs faster AI investment while dealing with high energy costs, fragmented capital markets, slow permitting and a higher global cost of capital. The priority is simple: *one capital market, integrated energy, faster permitting, AI factories, grid investment, industrial AI*.

Europe's strongest opportunity remains the physical economy: mobility, railways, manufacturing, energy, robotics, logistics and infrastructure.

::pull THE VELOCITY INSIGHT
The AI Supercycle is not slowing. It is becoming more expensive. Technology determines what is possible. Infrastructure determines scale. Capital determines endurance. ==Profitable velocity determines the winner.==

::takeaways Five Investor Takeaways | positioning for a 5% world
- **Favor scarcity over generic AI exposure.** Accelerated computing, memory, networking, optics, power and grid infrastructure retain structural pricing power.
- **Watch credit as closely as equities.** Financing costs are becoming a leading indicator of how far the Supercycle can scale.
- **Separate demand growth from return on capital.** Explosive usage does not guarantee attractive economics if debt, power and infrastructure costs rise faster than monetization.
- **Increase attention to agentic infrastructure.** Persistent agents could multiply inference demand far faster than user growth alone suggests.
- **Own the businesses that can compound through a 5% world.** The strongest positions combine scarcity assets, contracted demand, pricing power, high utilization and low relative funding costs.

::close The next winners will not simply accelerate faster. They will ==accelerate profitably==.
