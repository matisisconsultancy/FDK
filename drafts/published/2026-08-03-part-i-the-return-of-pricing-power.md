Today's session extended the recent improvement in global risk sentiment. Oil prices retreated further from their recent highs, sovereign bond markets stabilized, and equity indices continued to grind higher as investors interpreted the latest geopolitical developments as reducing the probability of an immediate supply shock.

Yet beneath today's relatively calm market action, something far more structural continued to unfold. Capital is no longer simply rotating between sectors. ==It is rotating between economic regimes.==

The market increasingly differentiates between companies capable of compounding productivity through Industrial AI and those whose earnings remain tied to traditional economic cycles. The distinction is becoming less about technology and increasingly about **future cash-flow durability**. The repricing continues.

::pull FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
What if today's rally had less to do with confidence — and more to do with investors accepting that the Age of Industrial AI is fundamentally repricing the global economy?

::signals Five Key Signals | what today's tape is really saying

# Energy Risk Premium Continues to Compress
Oil prices eased again as markets assigned a lower probability to a near-term disruption in global energy supplies. Markets are increasingly treating geopolitical events as temporary volatility rather than permanent inflation shocks.
signal: Geopolitics is being priced as volatility, not a permanent inflation shock.

# AI Infrastructure Remains the Strongest Capital Magnet
Demand for accelerated computing, hyperscale AI infrastructure, memory, networking, power equipment and industrial software continues to attract disproportionate capital flows.
signal: The investment cycle remains intact.

# Bond Markets Still Signal Discipline
Long-term yields remain elevated enough to remind investors that financing conditions have not returned to the ultra-cheap world of the previous decade.
signal: Capital is becoming more selective.

# Europe Continues to Search for Growth
European equities benefited from improving global sentiment, but structural growth expectations remain substantially below those embedded in US AI leaders.
signal: The valuation gap increasingly reflects differences in expected productivity growth.

# Industrial Companies Are Quietly Becoming AI Companies
The strongest performers increasingly combine software, automation, robotics, power electronics, semiconductors and industrial intelligence.
signal: Industrial AI is no longer a future theme — it is today's earnings story.

::section The Hidden Pattern | who owns the infrastructure of intelligence

Markets are no longer debating whether AI will transform the economy. They are now pricing **who owns the infrastructure of intelligence**.

The first phase rewarded software. The second rewarded chips. The third rewarded hyperscalers. The fourth — which has now begun — is rewarding the owners of the entire Industrial AI ecosystem.

::takeaways Key takeaways | reading the regime shift
- Intelligence is becoming industrial infrastructure — and infrastructure attracts capital at extraordinary scale.
- The market now differentiates by future cash-flow durability, not by technology labels.
- Elevated long-term yields keep capital selective even as risk sentiment improves.
- The US–Europe valuation gap reflects expected productivity growth, not sentiment alone.

::pull VELOCITY INSIGHT
The next bull market will not be defined by who builds better software. It will be defined by who owns the infrastructure through which intelligence is produced, distributed, and monetized.

::close Today's session looked quiet. History rarely announces itself with volatility — more often it appears as an ordinary trading day during which ==capital quietly changes direction==. That is precisely what continues to happen.
