The market is watching Italy's spread as a measure of sovereign risk. The bigger risk is that it becomes a measure of Italy's capacity to compete.

At 131 basis points, that transition has not happened. But the next 13 basis points could tell us whether it is beginning.

The thesis is not that Italy is approaching another sovereign crisis. It is that Italy could enter the most capital-intensive technology cycle in a generation with a rising relative cost of capital. That would matter far beyond government bonds.

::stats
131 bps :: Current BTP–Bund spread
136 bps :: Italy begins separating from Spain and France
144 bps :: Italy-specific premium propagates into banks and credit
~5% :: 10-year BTP level where financing economics begin to change

::section The Supercycle Is Becoming Physical | Why the spread acquires new meaning
The AI Supercycle is becoming physical. Compute requires data centers. Data centers require power. Power requires generation, grids and storage. Industrial AI requires factories, robotics, automation and digital infrastructure.

The competitive advantage increasingly belongs to economies capable of financing all of that at scale, quickly and cheaply.

This is where BTP–Bund acquires a new meaning. In 2011, the spread measured the market's confidence in Italy's ability to finance accumulated liabilities. In the next cycle, a persistent Italy-specific premium could increasingly measure the cost of financing new productive capacity.

::signals The Four Stages of Risk | Only one is specifically Italian
# European stress
The first stage is broad-based repricing across the continent. If the chain stops here, this remains a Europe trade.
signal: 131 asks whether the repricing persists.

# Italian differentiation
The second stage is Italy separating from its peers. This is the first stage that is specifically Italian.
signal: 136 asks whether Italy begins separating from Spain and France.

# Financial transmission
The third stage turns sovereign risk into a domestic financing problem as banks and credit follow.
signal: 144 asks whether that Italy-specific premium begins propagating into banks and credit.

# Capital-formation pressure
The fourth stage threatens Italy's position in the AI investment cycle, as infrastructure hurdle rates rise. A 10-year BTP approaching 5% is a separate overlay: it asks whether relative disadvantage is colliding with an absolute financing cost high enough to begin changing investment economics. That is the inflection point.
signal: The corridor is a disciplined way to identify whether the identity and consequence of the risk are changing.

::patterns What Kind of Trade Is This? | The chain reaction mapped

# Italian differentiation

::takeaways Key takeaways | Why 131 → 136 → 144 matters
- Thirteen basis points do not separate stability from crisis — the corridor measures whether the *nature* of the risk is changing.
- Only Italian differentiation is specifically Italian; only transmission makes it a domestic financing problem; only capital-formation pressure threatens Italy's AI position.
- A 10-year BTP approaching 5% is the inflection point where relative disadvantage meets absolute cost.
- The market does not need another debt crisis for this to matter — only for the premium to begin changing where the next euro of productive capital is deployed.
