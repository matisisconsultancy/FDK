Trump–Xi lowered geopolitical risk. It did not lower the hurdle rate.

The Washington meeting points toward managed competition, not convergence. The U.S. and China have more room to contain escalation, while the structural contest over AI, semiconductors, critical minerals and industrial capacity continues.

Meanwhile, the U.S. 10-year Treasury touched 5.23%. That is tonight's decisive signal: *AI no longer needs to prove that demand exists. It needs to prove that demand can generate returns above the cost of capital.*

::stats
5.23% :: U.S. 10-year Treasury yield touched
$11.6B :: Anthropic's seven-year Akamai commitment
2% → 5.2% :: From buying the promise to demanding proof

::pull PROOF IS THE NEW PREMIUM
At 2% money, markets bought the promise. At 5.2%, they demand proof — capital is not leaving AI, it is moving toward bankable AI.

::signals Five Signals | what the tape is telling us
# Capital is expensive
At 5.23%, distant cash flows lose value. Time-to-cash now matters as much as growth.
signal: The discount rate has become the story.

# AI capex is still accelerating
U.S. orders for computers and communications equipment remain exceptionally strong. The Supercycle is becoming physical.
signal: Demand is real and turning into steel and silicon.

# Contracts beat forecasts
Anthropic's $11.6 billion, seven-year Akamai commitment captures the new model: **CONTRACT → CAPACITY → UTILIZATION → CASH FLOW**.
signal: Underwriting beats storytelling.

# Trump–Xi changes the temperature, not the structure
Washington reduces near-term escalation risk. But two increasingly complete AI-industrial stacks continue to emerge.
signal: Détente on the surface, bifurcation underneath.

# The market is becoming selective
Capital is not leaving AI. It is moving toward bankable AI.
signal: Selectivity is the regime.

::section The New Hierarchy | from promise to proof
The value chain now reads as a descent from claims to cash:

Announced capacity → Contracted capacity → Energized capacity → Cash-generating capacity.

At 2% money, markets bought the promise. At 5.2%, they demand proof.

::section After Washington | managed bifurcation
The U.S.–China relationship is moving toward managed bifurcation.

**United States:** frontier models + accelerated computing + hyperscaler capital.

**China:** domestic models + semiconductors + memory + power + industrial scale.

The strategic consequence for Europe is unequivocal: ==Europe needs a third stack — not a third model.== Industrial AI. Power. Compute. Capital. Critical infrastructure.

::takeaways Key Takeaways | scale alone no longer wins
- **Investors:** favor contracted demand, secured power and visible cash flow.
- **CEOs:** measure CAPEX-to-Cash Velocity.
- **Europe:** build Industrial AI around its physical economy.
- **Markets:** watch the 10-year Treasury, AI credit spreads and energized capacity.
- **The regime:** scale alone no longer wins. Bankable scale does.

::close The AI Supercycle has reached the underwriting desk — the next Great Split is ==Bankable AI== versus AI that still needs to be believed.
