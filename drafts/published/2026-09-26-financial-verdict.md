The AI Supercycle remains intact. The free-capital era does not. At a ~5.2% risk-free rate, scale without returns destroys value.

The next re-rating will separate AI cash generators from AI capital consumers.

::stats
~5.2% :: Risk-free rate resetting the cost of scale
∞ → 0 :: The free-capital era, now closed
2 :: Cohorts the next re-rating will divide

::pull THE DIVIDE
Scale without returns destroys value. The market will no longer pay for growth that does not pay for itself.

::signals What the re-rating rewards | how capital re-sorts under a 5.2% rate
# Cash generators earn the premium
The AI names that convert intelligence into compounding capital will command the re-rating. Returns above the risk-free rate justify the scale behind them.
signal: Compounding beats scale when capital is no longer free.

# Capital consumers face the cut
Intelligence that consumes capital without returning it no longer survives a 5.2% hurdle. The subsidy of free money is gone.
signal: Growth that does not pay for itself gets re-priced down.

::takeaways The rule for investors and boards | simple, and non-negotiable
- Fund intelligence that compounds capital.
- Cut intelligence that consumes it.
- Judge every AI dollar against a ~5.2% risk-free hurdle.

::close The Supercycle survives. ==Free capital does not.==
