EuroHPC procurement: the official tender remains open. The latest published update is still dated November 5, 2026.

::signal
- **EuroHPC tender remains open**, with the latest published update still dated November 5, 2026.
- **Deadlines hold**: December 3, 2026 for large-scale and February 4, 2027 for medium-scale Gigafactories, both 17:00 CET.
- **No replacement submission links** have been published following the technical problem affecting existing applications.
- **November 7 demonstrations** in Spain, France, Italy and the Nordics produced no moratorium or verified project cancellation.
- **Anthropic's ~$3.4bn gas-generator commitment** for 1.7 GW was reported November 8 but does not meet today's alert criteria.

The deadlines remain December 3, 2026, for large-scale Gigafactories and February 4, 2027, for medium-scale Gigafactories, both at 17:00 CET. EuroHPC has not yet published replacement submission links following the technical problem affecting existing applications.

::section Developments assessed but not elevated to an alert | What we watched and why it falls short
The November 7 European demonstrations against data-center expansion included planned actions in Spain, France, Italy and the Nordics. No resulting national moratorium or verified material project cancellation was identified.

Separately, Reuters reported on November 8 that Anthropic had arranged approximately $3.4 billion of gas-generator commitments for 1.7 GW of capacity. The underlying transaction reportedly occurred in September and does not establish a new European investment or power commitment. Its implications for electricity procurement remain relevant to longer-term competitive analysis, but it does not meet the geographic and materiality criteria for today's alert.

::close
The next decisive indicators remain ==EuroHPC's replacement submission links==, binding consortium and national financing commitments, enforceable electricity connections, and independently confirmed construction or commissioning milestones.
