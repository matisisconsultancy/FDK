The competition is no longer principally about models, chips or Gigafactories. It is about who can turn capital and energy into intelligence—and intelligence into productivity, cash flow and greater technological power.

The United States has a system. China is building another. Europe has the components. It does not yet have the system.

::pull THE FLYWHEEL
CAPITAL → POWER → COMPUTE → INDUSTRIAL AI → PRODUCTIVITY → CASH FLOW → CAPITAL

::section From Models to Systems | how the race evolved
The AI race has evolved: **MODELS → COMPUTE → INFRASTRUCTURE → SYSTEMS.**

A more managed U.S.–China rivalry could actually accelerate investment on both sides: Risk ↓ → AI CAPEX ↑ → Scale ↑ → Ecosystem Depth ↑.

Europe is therefore not competing against today's gap. It is competing against the speed at which that gap compounds.

::section From Gigafactories to Compounding MW | owning the economics
A Gigafactory creates capacity. The real objective is to create an economic flywheel: POWER → COMPUTE → INDUSTRIAL AI → PRODUCTIVITY → CASH FLOW → REINVESTMENT.

Call it ==Compounding MW==: AI capacity that generates the demand and capital required to finance its own expansion. That is the difference between hosting AI infrastructure and owning the economics of AI.

::pull VALUE CAPTURE
Location is not sovereignty. Value capture is.

::patterns Europe's Edge | components in search of a system
# Capital + Low-Carbon Power
Europe should not replicate America or China. Its advantage begins with abundant capital and low-carbon power feeding the flywheel.

# Grids + Telecoms + Engineering
Distribution and engineering depth can turn raw compute into contracted, continental-scale demand.

# Manufacturing + Robotics + Mobility
The opportunity is Industrial AI at continental scale—Gigafactories are not the destination, they are the infrastructure of the flywheel.

::section The Velocity Insight | the real question
Europe's question is no longer: *Can we build enough Gigafactories?*

It is: *Can Europe build a system where capital creates intelligence, intelligence creates productivity, and productivity finances more intelligence?* That is how compute becomes economic power.

::takeaways Key takeaways | for investors and policymakers
- **Investors — Move beyond MW:** focus on cash flow per AI MW.
- **Investors — Prioritize Compounding MW:** power + compute + customers + applications.
- **Investors — Follow the bottlenecks:** grids, storage, cooling, photonics and fiber.
- **Investors — Watch telecoms:** distribution can turn compute into contracted demand.
- **Policymakers — Measure European value captured per MW,** not MW alone.
- **Policymakers — Connect Gigafactories** directly to Industrial AI demand.
- **Policymakers — Use public procurement** to crowd in private capital.
- **Policymakers — Integrate AI + energy + semiconductors + telecoms + capital markets** into one strategy.
- **Policymakers — Build a continental system,** not 27 national ones.

::close The endpoint of the AI race is not the model, the GPU or the Gigafactory. It is the system that turns intelligence into capital—and capital into more intelligence. America has one. China is building another. ==Europe has a deadline.==
