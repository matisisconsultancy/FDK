Material inflection: yes. The latest evidence sharpens our thesis: **America leads the AI frontier. China is building the largest AI diffusion engine.**

China installed 354,000 industrial robots in 2025—59% of the global total and more than 9× U.S. installations. Chinese suppliers also captured 55% of their domestic market.

The significance is not the robots themselves. It is the infrastructure they create for Physical AI. As intelligence moves from software into machines, China already possesses the manufacturing base, installed equipment and deployment scale onto which AI can be layered.

::stats
354,000 :: Industrial robots installed by China in 2025
59% :: Share of global robot installations
9× :: China's lead over U.S. installations
55% :: Domestic market captured by Chinese suppliers

::pull The strategic question
The strategic question is shifting from *who builds the best model?* to ==who deploys intelligence into the physical economy fastest?==

::patterns The Great Split | Two paths through the Supercycle
# United States
Frontier models → advanced silicon → hyperscale compute → higher-value intelligence.
# China
Efficient AI → robots → factories → industrial scale → mass diffusion.

::section Why It Matters for the AI Supercycle | Physical AI broadens the perimeter
Physical AI expands the Supercycle beyond data centers: better models → cheaper inference → smarter machines → more deployment → more edge compute, sensors, memory, networking and power.

China's manufacturing scale becomes an AI advantage precisely as machine vision, cheaper inference and more capable models expand the economic use cases for robotics. The AI infrastructure opportunity therefore **broadens rather than contracts**.

::takeaways Key takeaways | Where the advantage sits
- America still leads the frontier. Advanced models and compute remain the core U.S. advantage.
- China increasingly leads deployment scale. 59% of global robot installations is industrial infrastructure, not experimentation.
- Physical AI is the next battleground. China's manufacturing installed base creates a powerful deployment advantage.
- The investment perimeter expands. AI increasingly means not only GPUs and data centers, but robotics, sensors, edge compute, networking, storage and electricity.

::pull The Velocity Insight
America is raising the capability ceiling. China is scaling the deployment floor. The next phase will increasingly be decided by ==Intelligence × Machines × Deployment Velocity==.

::close The frontier creates intelligence. ==Physical AI turns it into industrial power.==
