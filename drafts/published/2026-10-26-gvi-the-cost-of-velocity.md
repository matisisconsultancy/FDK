The emerging Global Velocity divide is between economies that turn progress into financially sustainable capacity and those whose acceleration depends on fragile financing, energy and institutional conditions.

::section The Hidden Pattern — Financial Velocity Becomes the Constraint | intelligence to cash flow
Technological acceleration raises potential capacity, but capturing it requires investment, energy, resilience and sustainable financing. The mechanism runs: intelligence → deployment → productivity → cash flow → reinvestment, each stage carrying a different constraint. A country with extraordinary computing investment but weak returns holds no sustainable velocity advantage. The decisive distinction is between technological expansion and economically productive acceleration.

- Investors — Follow cash conversion; differentiate AI revenue growth from sustainable free cash flow.
