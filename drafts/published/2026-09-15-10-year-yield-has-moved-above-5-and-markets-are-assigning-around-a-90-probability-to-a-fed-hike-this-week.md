Higher rates initially support banking margins. But they also raise funding costs, increase credit risk, compress valuation multiples and raise the hurdle rate for every acquisition.

The *Risiko* is therefore becoming simultaneously more strategic and more financially demanding.

::pull VELOCITY INSIGHT
The Italian Banking Paradox is revealing a deeper principle of modern financial competition: ==share currency is strategic power==.

::section The Equation | What actually decides control

Balance-sheet scale matters. Capital matters. Politics matters. Regulatory approvals matter.

But in an all-share consolidation battle, the ability to sustain a valuable equity currency can determine which institution is able to transform strategic intent into control.

That gives us today's equation:

**EQUITY CURRENCY × TIME × COALITION SUPPORT × POLITICAL EXECUTABILITY = STRATEGIC OPTIONALITY**

::takeaways Key takeaways | The new battlefield
- Higher rates cut both ways — supporting margins while raising funding costs, credit risk and every acquisition's hurdle rate.
- The next phase will not be won by the institution willing to pay the highest price.
- It will be won by the institution that can maintain the strongest currency of strategic credibility long enough for its preferred architecture to become irreversible.
