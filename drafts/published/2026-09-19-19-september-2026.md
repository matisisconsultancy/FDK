The U.S.–China AI race is entering a more consequential phase. The central question is no longer simply who has the best frontier model. It is becoming: who controls the architecture through which intelligence is produced, powered, distributed and adopted?

The latest signals reinforce our earlier Full-Stack Control thesis. But there is an important evolution today: AI itself is moving directly onto the U.S.–China negotiating table.

::stats

6 :: Chinese AI firms accused of distillation
Open + Closed :: Model classes now on the table
Stage III :: The System Race begins

::pull THE BATTLE
The contest is migrating upstream and downstream simultaneously: Minerals → Energy → Chips → Compute → Models → Applications → Distribution.

::section AI Becomes Geopolitical Infrastructure | The negotiating table
U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is expected to discuss AI, rare earths, trade and economic issues with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng this weekend, ahead of the Trump–Xi meeting scheduled for September 24. Significantly, Bessent indicated that the discussions will cover both open- and closed-weight models.

That pairing matters. AI + rare earths means that Washington and Beijing are increasingly treating the AI stack as one interconnected strategic system.

::signals The Two Architectures | How each side compounds
# China: Compensate for Compute With Diffusion
China continues to face constraints in access to the most advanced foreign compute. Huawei is therefore becoming increasingly important to the domestic ecosystem; recent reporting points toward a significant migration of Chinese AI training toward Huawei hardware.

But China possesses another lever: open-weight diffusion. Chinese models are increasingly attractive internationally because they can be downloaded, modified and deployed at relatively low cost. Reuters reports that Chinese open-weight models are gaining traction even among American companies.
signal: China may not need to reproduce the American stack exactly — it can route around its bottlenecks.

# America: Capital Intensity Becomes a Weapon
The American chain runs almost in the opposite direction: frontier models demand exponentially more compute, accelerated computing, data-center expansion, power and grid infrastructure, and enormous capital formation — which feeds back into model capability.

An important new constraint is appearing: electricity availability. Google, Nvidia and Anthropic have joined an initiative with Emerald AI aimed at making data-center electricity demand more flexible, arguing that demand-response techniques could allow very substantial additional capacity onto constrained grids.
signal: The marginal constraint on AI is moving from silicon toward the physical economy.

# A New Front: Distillation
U.S. authorities this month accused six Chinese AI companies — including DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Alibaba — of using American model outputs in what officials characterize as industrial-scale distillation of proprietary capabilities. The companies' responses and the broader legal status of model distillation must be distinguished from the government's allegations.

Strategically, the issue is larger than the allegations. It exposes a fundamental problem with frontier AI: how do you contain an intelligence advantage when intelligence can teach another machine?
signal: AI controls must address capabilities, weights, outputs and knowledge transfer — making containment considerably harder.

::pull THE PARADOX
America leads the frontier; China attacks the cost curve. A model that is marginally weaker but dramatically cheaper and easier to deploy can generate ==enormous economic influence==.

::patterns The Three Stages of the Race | The unit of competition changes
# [I] Model Race
GPT vs DeepSeek vs Qwen vs Gemini vs Claude.

# [II] Compute Race
GPUs → accelerators → HBM → networking → data centers.

# [III] System Race
Energy + Compute + Models + Capital + Distribution + Industrial Deployment. It is no longer OpenAI vs DeepSeek, nor even Nvidia vs Huawei — increasingly it becomes American AI System vs Chinese AI System.

::section The Two Velocity Loops | Capability vs diffusion
🇺🇸 **United States — Capability Compounding:** Capital → Accelerated Computing → Frontier Models → Agents → Enterprise Adoption → Cash Flow → More Capital.

🇨🇳 **China — Diffusion Compounding:** Constraints → Efficiency → Open Models → Low Cost → Developer Adoption → Industrial Deployment → Scale → More Optimization.

The first system attempts to maximize intelligence per frontier model. The second increasingly has incentives to maximize intelligence per dollar, watt and user.

::takeaways Key takeaways | Velocity Insight
- The Great Split is becoming architectural, not merely competitive.
- America's strongest advantage remains its concentration of capital, frontier laboratories, advanced compute and hyperscale infrastructure.
- China's strategic opportunity is different: compress the cost of intelligence and propagate it faster.
- The strategic contest can no longer be measured only with benchmark scores.

::close Does advantage accrue to the system that creates the most powerful intelligence — or to the one that makes sufficiently powerful intelligence ==ubiquitous first==? The answer may determine which technology stack becomes the operating system of the next global economy.
