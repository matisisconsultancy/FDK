AI is no longer just consuming capital. It is repricing it. The AI Supercycle is entering its harder financial phase, and scarcity has migrated from Compute → Energy → Capital.

The market has spent three years rewarding the scale of AI investment. It is now beginning to ask a more demanding question: what return will that capital earn? The next AI trade is not more capex — it is the spread between incremental ROIC and WACC.

::pull THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
What happens when the world's fastest investment cycle collides with a rising cost of capital? The feedback loop is now visible: AI Capex ↑ → Capital Demand ↑ → Yields/WACC ↑ → Required Returns ↑ → Capital Discipline ↑.

::signals The Transmission | five forces reshaping the trade
# The Next Bottleneck Is the Balance Sheet
Hyperscaler gross debt issuance could reach $420 billion in 2027, while AI-linked borrowers already trade at wider spreads than the broader investment-grade market. The first phase of AI was financed from extraordinary Big Tech cash generation. The next phase requires data centers, power, grids, storage, cooling, fiber and land — and the financing stack is becoming as important as the computing stack.

Strong balance sheets can lock in power, secure capacity, fund through the cycle and exploit weaker competitors. Leveraged participants face the opposite recursion: Higher Capex → More Debt → Higher Funding Cost → Higher Required ROIC.
signal: The bond market may impose capital discipline before the equity market does.

# Energy Enters the Discount Rate
Brent remains near $107/bbl as disruption around Hormuz forces energy through less efficient, more expensive routes. Oil is the first-order effect; the second-order transmission is more consequential — Energy → Inflation → Rates → WACC → Valuation, while simultaneously Energy → Electricity → Data-Center Opex → AI Margins.

Energy hits AI economics from both sides of the valuation equation: cash flows and discount rates. Expect power security to acquire an explicit valuation premium as strategic value migrates upstream — Nuclear → Grid → Storage → Power Equipment → Cooling → Compute.
signal: The cheapest reliable megawatt is becoming part of the technology stack.

# Central Banks — The AI Duration Problem
The U.S. 10-year Treasury recently moved above 5.1% as markets priced additional tightening. AI creates an unusual policy problem. Today: Capex ↑ → Construction ↑ → Power Demand ↑ → Credit Demand ↑ → Inflationary Pressure ↑. Potentially tomorrow: Automation ↑ → Productivity ↑ → Unit Costs ↓ → Potential Growth ↑. The issue is the lag between the two.

If productivity accelerates quickly, higher AI investment can support margins, potential growth and disinflation. If it lags, markets finance long-duration cash flows at high-duration discount rates. The emerging contest is Productivity Velocity vs. Hurdle-Rate Velocity.
signal: Time to productivity is becoming a financial variable.

# Europe — From AI Sovereignty to Industrial ROIC
The AI race is moving from models into the physical economy, and that changes Europe's relative position. Europe is unlikely to outspend America in frontier models or China in state-directed investment, but it owns increasingly strategic assets: industrial capacity, engineering, energy systems, mobility, robotics, aerospace and proprietary manufacturing data.

The relevant chain is Compute → Energy → Industrial Data → Automation → Productivity → ROIC. The investment test is whether European companies can translate AI deployment into higher asset utilization, lower unit costs, operating leverage, higher margins and higher ROIC.
signal: Europe does not need the largest AI capex cycle — it needs the highest industrial return on AI capital.

# The Next Market Rotation
Markets have already traded two stages of the Supercycle: AI 1.0 — Models + GPUs, and AI 2.0 — Data Centers + Infrastructure. The third phase is emerging: AI 3.0 — Returns. The market should increasingly demand the complete transmission: Capex → Utilization → Revenue → Margins → Free Cash Flow → ROIC. Every missing link becomes a source of de-rating risk.

The Great Split moves inside AI itself. On one side: high utilization, pricing power, operating leverage, internally funded capex. On the other: high capital intensity, refinancing dependence, weak utilization, distant cash flows.
signal: This is not the end of the AI trade — it is the beginning of capital rationing inside it.

::section The Hidden Pattern | three scarcities, one loop
Three scarcities have converged: Compute × Energy × Capital. More compute requires more electricity; more electricity requires more infrastructure; more infrastructure requires more capital; more capital demand raises the hurdle rate; and a higher hurdle rate demands higher incremental returns.

The loop becomes: AI Scale → Capex → Financing → Higher WACC → Higher Required ROIC → Capital Reallocation → Valuation Dispersion. Geopolitics reinforces it: Fragmentation → Energy Scarcity → Inflation → Higher Rates → Higher WACC → Higher AI Hurdle Rate.

As capital becomes more expensive, weaker projects are rationed out and capital concentrates around businesses with superior economics. The AI Supercycle is becoming its own capital-allocation mechanism — and the result may be more concentration, not less.

::patterns The AI Spread | expanding, compressing, negative
# [+] Expanding Spread
Incremental ROIC running above WACC brings multiple expansion, tighter credit and capital inflows.

# [=] Compressing Spread
ROIC converging toward WACC brings de-rating, wider credit and capital rationing.

# [–] Negative Spread
ROIC below WACC means value destruction.

::pull VELOCITY INSIGHT
INCREMENTAL ROIC − WACC = THE NEW AI SPREAD. The rotation is Capex → Utilization → ROIC → Free Cash Flow → Repricing. Watch the spread — not the spend.

::takeaways Key takeaways for investors | the ROIC phase
- The AI trade becomes a ROIC trade — capex growth matters less unless incremental returns exceed the rising cost of capital.
- Own scarcity with pricing power: power, grids, nuclear, memory, networking and cooling.
- Balance sheets become strategic assets; self-funded AI investment deserves a rising premium.
- Prepare for greater dispersion between high-ROIC infrastructure and capital-intensive projects with distant paybacks.
- Watch AI credit spreads, hyperscaler power contracts, utilization rates, free-cash-flow conversion and productivity data.
