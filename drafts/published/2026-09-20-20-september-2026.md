The AI race is no longer about building intelligence. It is about making intelligence pay. Models are improving and compute is expanding, but the scarce inputs are moving outward — from memory to power to grid to capital to materials to permission.

The U.S. 10-year Treasury breached 5% this week before retreating below it. So 5% is not today's fixed yield; it is the benchmark around which long-duration capital is being repriced. At the same time, Brent remains above $100, while Nscale reports more than $103B of contracted AI value and a power pipeline above 10 GW.

::stats

3.75%–4.00% :: Fed policy rate
>$100/bbl :: Brent crude
>$103B :: Nscale contracted AI value

::pull THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
Who can scale AI and still earn a sustainable return above its full cost of capital?

::section The 5% Benchmark | three numbers, three meanings
Three numbers carry three distinct meanings. The Fed Funds rate at 3.75%–4.00% is the short-term policy rate. The 10Y Treasury near a 5% benchmark is the long-duration sovereign reference rate. The AI WACC is higher still and project-specific.

Therefore: **5% Treasury ≠ AI WACC ≠ AI hurdle rate.** The economic test is ROIC > WACC — and the wider the sustainable spread, the stronger the value creation.

::section Why Nscale Matters | neocloud economics under test
Nscale is a live test of neocloud economics. The demand question is increasingly answered; the conversion test is the chain from contracts to commissioned MW to utilization to EBITDA to FCF to ROIC.

::pull NSCALE
Nscale is a test of whether gigawatts can become returns.

::section Europe — The Second AI Trade | own the physical stack
Europe's opportunity increasingly sits below the model layer: semiconductor equipment; grids; electrical equipment; nuclear; industrial automation; mobility infrastructure.

Europe may have missed part of the model trade. It does not have to miss the infrastructure trade. The investor question: who owns the electrons, equipment and industrial systems AI cannot scale without?

::patterns Investor Action — Three Steps | screen, own, avoid
# [1] Screen — Buy the Spread
Start with ROIC − WACC. If the spread is not sustainably positive, stop there.

# [2] Own — The Bottleneck + The Conversion
Favor assets combining scarcity, pricing power, secured energy, credible delivery, high utilization and strong balance sheets. Follow the conversion chain: backlog → capacity → utilization → cash flow → returns. For Europe, prioritize the physical stack.

# [3] Avoid — Capital Without Productivity
Penalize refinancing dependence, unsecured power, grid delays, customer concentration, low utilization and weak cash conversion. Capacity that cannot earn its cost of capital is not an AI asset — it is a capital liability.

::takeaways Key takeaways | the hierarchy
- **Screen the spread.** ROIC must sit sustainably above WACC.
- **Own the bottleneck.** Scarcity, secured energy and pricing power.
- **Demand the conversion.** Backlog must become cash flow and returns.
- The supercycle is intact — but the cost-of-capital regime has changed.
