TSMC continues to report exceptional revenue momentum tied to advanced-compute demand, and memory markets remain supported by AI infrastructure requirements. Upstream demand is intact.

But large-scale AI deployment is increasingly reaching beyond conventional capex into leases, supplier finance, structured vehicles and long-duration infrastructure commitments. Frontier-AI companies now confront two financial questions simultaneously: how revenue should be economically attributed, and how increasingly large compute commitments should be financed.

The FDK core judgment is that the AI cycle is moving from a capacity test to an economics test. The benchmark reduces that test to one chain: ==revenue ownership → capital burden → cash conversion → incremental ROIC==.

::pull Capital Discipline

# Frontier-AI Economics Are Becoming More Visible
OpenAI revenue reporting has highlighted differences between headline annualized run rates and economically comparable revenue. Anthropic's infrastructure expansion illustrates the scale of capital commitments required to compete at the frontier.
signal: The comparison is no longer growth alone, but economically attributable revenue relative to compute burden and cash generation.

# Financing Is Entering the AI Value Chain
Leases, SPVs, supplier finance and contractual infrastructure commitments are being used more extensively across the AI ecosystem.
signal: Financing structure is now part of the competitive model.

::pull The Core Divergence
Leaders invest to expand competitive options. Laggards still invest disproportionately to repair existing economics — and across the ten pillars, Capital Formation (↑↑) is accelerating faster than demonstrated Productivity Acceleration (→). That imbalance must close through stronger margins, FCF or incremental ROIC.

::patterns Second-Order Market Effects | How value redistributes
# Credit Becomes a Leading Indicator
Financing stress may appear before operating demand weakens.

# Supplier Multiples Track Customer ROIC

# Value Moves Downstream
As infrastructure matures, incremental value should migrate toward software, industrial automation, workflow platforms and vertical AI.

# Disclosure Quality Earns a Premium

# Power Becomes a Financial Variable

# Capital Intensity Drives Consolidation
