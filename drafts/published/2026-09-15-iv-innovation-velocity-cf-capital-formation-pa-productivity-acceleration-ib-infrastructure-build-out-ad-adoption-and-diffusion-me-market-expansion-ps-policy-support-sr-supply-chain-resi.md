Which industries are building the Next Economy fastest today? The Global Velocity Index (GVI) offers a ranked answer, and the leaders sit at the intersection of computing demand and industrial reality.

Semiconductors lead because computing demand is broadening across the entire silicon architecture — not only accelerators. Aerospace and defense move into second because orbital connectivity has become commercial infrastructure while sovereign procurement remains structurally funded.

::pull LEAD SIGNAL
The Next Economy is no longer being built fastest where intelligence is merely announced. It is being built where intelligence receives power, receives permission, becomes an industrial order — and earns a return.
