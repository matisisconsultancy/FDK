The central thesis is uncomfortable but simple: AI is already tightening the monetary and financial environment — before its full productivity dividend is visible.

The mechanism runs in two phases. First, the cost: AI capex drives power, compute and infrastructure demand, which creates financing pressure, higher real yields, higher hurdle rates and less room for easing. Only later comes the dividend: AI diffusion lifts output, lowers unit costs, delivers disinflation and restores room for easing.

Three central-bank signals now support that reading.

::pull The Message
The cost of building AI is arriving before the cost savings from using it.

::signals Central-Bank Signals | three channels, one message

# Fed — Rate Channel
Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack points to higher real yields as heavy technology investment intensifies financing demand. That demand is adding pressure while real yields remain elevated.
signal: Heavy technology investment is lifting financing demand into an environment of elevated real yields.

# Fed — Systemic-Risk Channel
Kansas City Fed President Jeff Schmid is asking whether the AI ecosystem itself could become systemically important, as it grows more concentrated, leveraged and interconnected.
signal: The AI ecosystem is concentrating, levering up and interconnecting — a systemic-risk question in the making.

# ECB — Macro-Scale Channel
The ECB sees AI effects across semiconductor constraints, investment and corporate financing — evidence the demand is visible at macro scale.
signal: AI demand shows up in chip constraints, investment and corporate financing across the euro area.

::section Europe's Test | absorbing the cost, capturing the gain

Europe risks absorbing higher financing costs, higher energy demand and technology dependence — without capturing enough productivity, margins, IP and cash flow.

The imperative is to compress the chain: capital → compute → adoption → productivity → cash flow.

::patterns Investor Takeaways | position for the cost phase

# Rates
Respect real yields; stay selective on duration.

# Infrastructure
Own power, grids, compute, cooling and semiconductors.

# Equities
Demand cash-flow conversion, not AI narrative.

# Credit
Reprice leverage and refinancing risk.

::takeaways Board Takeaways | govern the capex
- Raise the hurdle rate.
- Force CapEx accountability.
- Secure energy, compute, data and skills.
- Stress-test higher-for-longer real yields.
- Govern the conversion from AI spending to competitive advantage.

Rotation follows the same discipline: broaden only when unit costs fall and returns on capital rise.

::close Own the bottlenecks. Demand cash flow. Respect real yields. Govern the capex. ==The regime turns when AI stops absorbing capital — and starts releasing productivity.==
