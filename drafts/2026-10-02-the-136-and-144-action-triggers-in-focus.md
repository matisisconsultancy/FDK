The Spread Level Opens the Test. Cross-Market Confirmation Activates the Signal.
Neither 136 nor 144 should be traded mechanically.
The framework is:
LEVEL → CONFIRMATION → ACTION.
A threshold without confirmation is observation.
A threshold with confirmation is information.


⸻


136 — THE ITALY DIFFERENTIATION TRIGGER
When European Stress Becomes an Italy Trade
A BTP–Bund move through 136 bps opens the Italy test.
It does not complete it.
TRIGGER
BTP–Bund ≥ 136 bps
REQUIRED CONFIRMATION
At least one primary Italian cross-spread must widen, with the other not contradicting the signal:
BTP–Bonos wider
and preferably:
BTP–OAT wider.
The strongest configuration is:
**BTP–Bund ≥ 136
BTP–Bonos ↑
BTP–OAT ↑
Italian banks underperform European banks.**
ACTIONABLE INTERPRETATION
If confirmed:
Reclassify the move from European stress to Italian differentiation.
The market is now attaching an incremental risk premium specifically to Italy.
The investment question changes from:
How much is Europe repricing?
to:
How much additional financing disadvantage is Italy beginning to acquire versus Europe?
136 FAIL CONDITION
If BTP–Bund crosses 136 while:
**OAT–Bund ↑
Bonos–Bund ↑
BTP–Bonos broadly stable
BTP–OAT broadly stable,**
do not activate the Italy signal.
The move remains predominantly European.
136 REVERSAL CONDITION
If BTP–Bund subsequently moves back below the threshold and Italian cross-spreads retrace, the differentiation signal weakens.
Do not treat an intraday touch as a structural break.
Persistence matters.
136 ACTION RULE
136 alone = WATCH.
136 + wider BTP–Bonos/BTP–OAT = ITALY SIGNAL.
136 + cross-spread widening + Italian bank underperformance = STRONG ITALY SIGNAL.


⸻


144 — THE FINANCIAL TRANSMISSION TRIGGER
When the Italy Trade Becomes a Financing Trade
A move toward or through 144 bps opens a different test.
By this point, Italian differentiation should already have been established.
If it has not, 144 cannot be treated as an Italian transmission signal.
PREREQUISITE
Before activating the 144 framework:
BTP–Bonos must already be wider
and/or
BTP–OAT must already be wider.
In other words:
Italy must first separate from Europe.
Only then can we test whether that separation is transmitting.
TRIGGER
BTP–Bund ≈ 144 bps or higher
with Italian differentiation still intact.
REQUIRED CONFIRMATION
Now stop looking primarily at sovereign cross-spreads.
Move down the financing chain.
Watch:
Italian banks vs. European banks
Italian bank credit vs. European bank credit
Italian corporate credit vs. European corporate credit.
The strongest configuration is:
**BTP–Bund ≈ 144+
BTP–Bonos wider
BTP–OAT wider
Italian banks underperform
Italian credit underperforms.**
ACTIONABLE INTERPRETATION
If confirmed:
Reclassify the move from Italian sovereign differentiation to Italian financial transmission.
The sovereign premium is beginning to affect the domestic financing system.
The investment question changes again.
It is no longer:
Is Italy being repriced?
It becomes:
What Italian assets and projects will now face a higher cost of capital?


⸻


144 FAIL CONDITION
If BTP–Bund reaches 144 but:
**BTP–Bonos is stable
BTP–OAT is stable
Italian banks remain resilient relative to Europe
Italian credit shows no relative deterioration,**
do not call regime change.
The headline spread is wider.
But transmission is not confirmed.
144 without downstream confirmation remains a sovereign-market signal.


⸻


144 REVERSAL CONDITION
If Italian bank and credit underperformance reverses while sovereign cross-spreads tighten, the transmission thesis weakens.
The framework should therefore require persistence across markets, not a single intraday print.
The critical distinction is:
touching 144
versus
building a financing regime around 144.
The second is what matters.


⸻


144 ACTION RULE
144 alone = WATCH.
144 + confirmed Italian differentiation = TRANSMISSION TEST ACTIVE.
144 + Italian differentiation + bank underperformance = EARLY TRANSMISSION.144 + bank underperformance + Italian credit deterioration = TRANSMISSION CONFIRMED.


⸻


THE 5% ESCALATION
When Financial Transmission Becomes an Investment Test
If the 144 transmission signal is confirmed while the 10-year BTP approaches 5%, escalate the framework again.
Now Italy faces both:
relative financing disadvantage
and
high absolute financing cost.
The next assets to watch are no longer primarily sovereigns or banks.
They are:
utilities
grids
power generation
data centers
telecom infrastructure
industrial automation
long-duration corporate investment.
The question becomes:
Are project hurdle rates changing?
That is the point where the AI Supercycle enters the trade directly.

THE MONDAY RULE
The actionable hierarchy is therefore:
136 asks: Should we stop treating this as merely European?
144 asks: Should we stop treating this as merely sovereign?
That distinction makes the framework tradable without making it mechanical.
At 136, move from Europe to Italy only if the cross-spreads confirm.
At 144, move from Italy sovereigns to the financing system only if banks and credit confirm.
Near 5%, move from finance to capital formation only if investment-sensitive assets confirm.
The number opens the door.
Confirmation tells us whether to walk through it.
