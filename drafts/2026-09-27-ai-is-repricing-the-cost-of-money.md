BEFORE IT REPRICES THE COST OF PRODUCTION

27 September 2026 | FDK

QUICK TAKE

THE CENTRAL THESIS:

AI IS ALREADY TIGHTENING THE MONETARY AND FINANCIAL ENVIRONMENT — BEFORE ITS FULL PRODUCTIVITY DIVIDEND IS VISIBLE.

The mechanism is straightforward:

AI CAPEX
→ POWER + COMPUTE + INFRASTRUCTURE DEMAND
→ FINANCING PRESSURE
→ HIGHER REAL YIELDS
→ HIGHER HURDLE RATES
→ LESS ROOM FOR EASING

Only later:

AI DIFFUSION
→ HIGHER OUTPUT
→ LOWER UNIT COSTS
→ DISINFLATION
→ MORE ROOM FOR EASING

Three signals now support that thesis:

• Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack points to higher real yields as heavy technology investment intensifies financing demand.
• Kansas City Fed President Jeff Schmid is asking whether the AI ecosystem itself could become systemically important.
• The ECB sees AI effects across semiconductor constraints, investment and corporate financing.

CENTRAL-BANK SIGNALS

FED — RATE CHANNEL
Heavy technology investment is adding to financing demand while real yields remain elevated.

FED — SYSTEMIC-RISK CHANNEL
The AI ecosystem is becoming more concentrated, leveraged and interconnected.

ECB — MACRO-SCALE CHANNEL
AI demand is visible in semiconductor constraints, investment and corporate financing.

Combined message:

THE COST OF BUILDING AI IS ARRIVING BEFORE THE COST SAVINGS FROM USING IT.

EUROPE’S TEST

Europe risks absorbing:

HIGHER FINANCING COSTS

• HIGHER ENERGY DEMAND
• TECHNOLOGY DEPENDENCE

without capturing enough:

PRODUCTIVITY
MARGINS
IP
CASH FLOW

Europe must compress:

CAPITAL → COMPUTE → ADOPTION → PRODUCTIVITY → CASH FLOW

INVESTOR TAKEAWAYS

• RATES — Respect real yields; stay selective on duration.
• INFRASTRUCTURE — Own power, grids, compute, cooling and semiconductors.
• EQUITIES — Demand cash-flow conversion, not AI narrative.
• CREDIT — Reprice leverage and refinancing risk.
• ROTATION — Broaden only when unit costs fall and returns on capital rise.

BOARD TAKEAWAYS

• Raise the hurdle rate.
• Force CapEx accountability.
• Secure energy, compute, data and skills.
• Stress-test higher-for-longer real yields.
• Govern the conversion from AI spending to competitive advantage.

FINAL VERDICT

OWN THE BOTTLENECKS.
DEMAND CASH FLOW.
RESPECT REAL YIELDS.
GOVERN THE CAPEX.

THE REGIME TURNS WHEN AI STOPS ABSORBING CAPITAL — AND STARTS RELEASING PRODUCTIVITY.
