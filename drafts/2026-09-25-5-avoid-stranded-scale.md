.THE VELOCITY EDGE — THE MORNING VIEW

THE 5% RESET
The evidence is ordered by consequence. First, the U.S. 10-year Treasury is around 5.20% after touching 5.2251%, while the 30-year is near 5.48% after reaching 5.5016%—the highest levels in roughly two decades. Second, Brent remains near $105.6, keeping the energy-inflation channel open. Third, SoftBank has raised $11.1 billion to finance OpenAI at dollar coupons reaching 9.75%. Yet AI investment continues. (StreetInsider.com⁠)
The consequence is clear: AI growth now has to compete for capital on materially tougher terms.
⸻
The Fundamental Question
Can AI cash flows compound faster than the hurdle rate rising against them?
When medium-term U.S. sovereign debt yields roughly 5% before investors assume corporate or execution risk, every AI project, infrastructure asset and equity story must make a stronger case for capital.
⸻
Five Signals
1. Rates — From duration to discipline
Fact: U.S. long yields are at multi-decade highs. (StreetInsider.com⁠)
Shift: Long-duration growth is being repriced against a 5%+ sovereign benchmark.
Investment consequence: favor current earnings, pricing power and low refinancing dependence.
EARNINGS VISIBILITY > DISTANT OPTIONALITY
⸻
2. Energy — From input cost to valuation pressure
Fact: Brent is trading near $105.6/bbl, despite tentative hopes for U.S.–Iran progress. (Trading Economics⁠)
Shift: Energy remains high enough to sustain inflation and policy pressure.
Investment consequence: prefer energy-resilient assets; avoid low-margin businesses unable to pass through higher input costs.
ENERGY → INFLATION → RATES
⸻
3. AI — From capacity to monetization
Fact: SoftBank has issued $11.1 billion of bonds to fund OpenAI, with dollar tranches priced at 8.625%, 9.25% and 9.75%. (Investing.com⁠)
Shift: AI demand remains strong, but financing is becoming more selective.
Investment consequence: favor distribution, subscriptions, contracted demand and visible utilization over uncommitted capacity.
MONETIZATION > MEGAWATTS
⸻
4. U.S.–China — From decoupling to leverage
Fact: Washington and Beijing continue negotiations over AI, trade and critical inputs, while the broader relationship remains strategically interdependent rather than fully decoupled. (Reuters⁠)
Shift: Dependencies are increasingly being used as bargaining instruments.
Investment consequence: favor supply-chain redundancy and assets positioned at critical technological and material chokepoints.
INTERDEPENDENCE BECOMES LEVERAGE
⸻
5. Europe — From broad exposure to selective ownership
Fact: French 10-year borrowing costs now trade more than 110 basis points above Germany, the widest spread since the euro-area crisis, even as Europe continues investing in strategic infrastructure. (AOL.com⁠)
Shift: Europe is becoming more attractive by sector and less attractive as a single macro trade.
Investment consequence: favor grids, electrification, automation, semiconductor equipment, secure infrastructure, rail and infrastructure finance.
OWN THE ENABLERS, NOT THE FRAGILITY
⸻
The Hidden Pattern
Higher rates are not stopping AI investment; they are separating bankable growth from capital-intensive ambition.
⸻
Investor Action Hierarchy
ONE-LINE VERDICT
OWN WHAT AI CANNOT AVOID. THEN OWN WHAT MONETIZES IT. BUY OPTIONALITY LAST.
1 — CORE OVERWEIGHT: MANDATORY BOTTLENECKS
Semiconductors, HBM, memory, advanced networking, grids, firm power, resilient data centres.
Structural demand, difficult substitution, strongest pricing power.
2 — SELECTIVE OVERWEIGHT: MONETIZATION ENGINES
Agentic distribution, AI security, enterprise deployment, infrastructure finance.
Closest to customers, transactions and recurring revenue.
3 — SELECTIVE ONLY: FUNDED OPTIONALITY
Frontier models, new compute platforms, emerging AI infrastructure.
Require secured customers, strong balance sheets, visible utilization and credible unit economics.
4 — UNDERWEIGHT: DEPENDENT CAPACITY
Uncontracted compute, leveraged infrastructure, weak intermediaries and businesses reliant on external power or financing.
