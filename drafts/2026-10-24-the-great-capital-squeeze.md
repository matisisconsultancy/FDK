France escapes a downgrade. Credit markets tighten. Energy prices rise. The next market rotation will reward those who command capital—and punish those dependent on it.

II. THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
Can Europe finance its industrial future before rising sovereign borrowing costs begin undermining the growth required to sustain it?
Europe must finance energy security, defense, infrastructure modernization and Industrial AI simultaneously.
Yet financial fragmentation threatens the capital required to achieve those objectives.
The negative feedback mechanism is:
Sovereign stress → Higher financing costs → Lower investment → Weaker growth → Further sovereign deterioration.
The constructive alternative is equally clear.
Credibility → Capital formation → Productivity → Growth → Sovereign sustainability.
Europe’s challenge is to restore financial credibility without sacrificing productive investment.
