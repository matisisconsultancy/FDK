The AI trade moves from compute scarcity to capital efficiency.

CAPEX was the race. ROIC is the reckoning.

2 October 2026 | FDK

The Big Take

The first phase of the AI trade is ending.

Compute scarcity created the winners. Capital efficiency will separate them.

The U.S.–China contest is accelerating that transition. America still controls much of the frontier. China is attacking the economics of that advantage through cheaper models, domestic substitution, offshore compute access and alternative financing.

The market consequence is decisive:

AI CAPEX IS NO LONGER THE SIGNAL.

THE RETURN ON AI CAPEX IS.

The investment equation has moved from:

GPUs → Capacity → Valuation

to:

CAPEX → UTILIZATION → REVENUE → FREE CASH FLOW → ROIC

The next winners will not be those building the most compute.

They will be those making compute earn the most.

⸻

1. America: the capability bet

The American model is straightforward:

Capital → Frontier Compute → Superior Intelligence → Premium Workloads → Cash Flow → More Capital

Its advantage remains formidable: advanced silicon, hyperscale infrastructure, frontier models, software ecosystems, enterprise distribution and deep capital markets.

But that advantage is becoming extraordinarily expensive.

The financial question is no longer whether U.S. companies can keep spending.

They can.

It is:

CAN AI REVENUE GROW FASTER THAN AI CAPITAL EMPLOYED?

If yes, the American frontier becomes one of history’s great compounding machines.

If not, today’s infrastructure boom becomes tomorrow’s ROIC compression.

That is the American risk.

⸻

2. China: the efficiency bet

China is optimizing a different equation:

Efficient Models → Lower System Cost → Cheaper Intelligence → More Deployment → Higher Utilization

DeepSeek compresses inference economics.

Huawei attacks compute dependency.

Domestic memory and networking reduce system costs.

Offshore capacity can bridge remaining hardware gaps.

China does not need to maximize revenue per token.

It needs to maximize:

ECONOMIC OUTPUT PER DOLLAR OF AI CAPITAL.

America seeks higher value per unit of compute.

China seeks more utilization per unit of capital.

That is the financial Great Split.

⸻

3. Compute is becoming an asset class

This is the pivotal market development.

GPUs are migrating from technology equipment toward financeable productive infrastructure.

The relevant economics increasingly resemble:

Asset Cost
→ Financing Cost
→ Utilization
→ Contracted Revenue
→ Cash Flow
→ Residual Value.

That opens AI to:

private credit
→ infrastructure funds
→ sovereign capital
→ insurers
→ leasing vehicles.

The consequence is enormous:

THE AI SUPERCYCLE CAN NOW LEVER THE BALANCE SHEET OF THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM.

That increases the potential size and duration of the boom.

It also introduces the next risks:

leverage, refinancing, utilization and technological obsolescence.

AI is becoming a credit cycle as well as an equity cycle.

⸻

4. The market rotation has already begun conceptually

The first phase rewarded scarcity:

GPU → HBM → Networking → Data Centers.

The second rewards efficiency around scarcity:

Inference optimization
→ memory efficiency
→ networking utilization
→ cooling
→ power efficiency
→ workload orchestration.

The investment thesis therefore changes from:

OWN THE BOTTLENECK

to:

OWN WHO MAKES THE BOTTLENECK EARN MORE.

That is a much broader—and more selective—trade.

⸻

5. Intelligence deflation will redistribute AI profits

China’s biggest contribution to the global AI economy may ultimately be price compression.

As intelligence becomes cheaper:

Inference Price ↓
→ Consumption ↑↑
→ Model Margins ↓
→ Infrastructure Utilization ↑
→ Application Economics ↑.

This moves economic rents.

MODEL SCARCITY ↓

INFRASTRUCTURE VOLUME ↑

APPLICATION VALUE ↑

The biggest winners may therefore not necessarily be the companies producing the model.They may increasingly be the companies owning scarce infrastructure—or using commoditized intelligence to generate measurable productivity and cash flow.

⸻

6. The Great Split becomes a financial contest

🇺🇸 UNITED STATES

Frontier CAPEX → superior intelligence → premium workloads → higher revenue per compute unit

ROIC THROUGH CAPABILITY

🇨🇳 CHINA

Lower-cost stack → cheaper intelligence → mass deployment → higher utilization

ROIC THROUGH VOLUME

The decisive question is no longer:

Who wins AI?

It is:

WHICH SYSTEM CONVERTS AI CAPITAL INTO CASH FLOW FASTER?

That is the metric financial markets will eventually price.

⸻

The AI trade will increasingly split companies into two categories.

AI COMPOUNDERS

AI investment
→ productivity/revenue
→ free cash flow
→ reinvestment
→ higher ROIC.

AI CAPITAL TRAPS

AI investment

This distinction will matter more than whether a company calls itself an AI winner.

THE NEXT GREAT SPLIT IS INSIDE THE AI TRADE ITSELF.

KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR INVESTORS

1. The indiscriminate AI CAPEX trade is ending.
The next phase rewards returns, not announcements.

2. U.S. frontier leadership remains the strongest capability trade.
But its enormous capital intensity means monetization must now catch up with investment.

3. China is the global AI deflation engine.
It will pressure model pricing while expanding inference volumes.

4. Infrastructure remains structurally advantaged—but selection matters.
Favor assets with scarcity, utilization and pricing power across HBM, networking, optics, power and cooling.

5. Applications move up the value chain.
As models commoditize, economic rents migrate toward companies turning cheap intelligence into productivity.

6. Credit becomes the next frontier—and the next risk.
AI infrastructure financing can extend the cycle, but leverage will expose weak utilization quickly.

7. The valuation premium moves to capital efficiency.

The decisive metric becomes:

FREE CASH FLOW / AI CAPITAL EMPLOYED

⸻

THE VELOCITY INSIGHT

The first phase asked:

WHO CAN GET THE COMPUTE?

The second:

WHO CAN FINANCE IT?

The phase now beginning asks:

WHO CAN MAKE IT EARN?

America is betting that better intelligence commands higher returns.

China is betting that cheaper intelligence creates overwhelming volume.

One maximizes margin.

The other maximizes utilization.

Both expand the AI economy.

But markets will not reward both equally.

THE NEXT AI ROTATION WILL BE FROM CAPACITY TO CASH FLOW.

And that is the decisive financial conclusion:

CAPEX WAS THE RACE.

ROIC IS THE RECKONING.
