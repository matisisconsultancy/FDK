27 September 2026 | FDK

The Big Take

Material inflection: yes.

The latest evidence sharpens our thesis:

AMERICA LEADS THE AI FRONTIER.

CHINA IS BUILDING THE LARGEST AI DIFFUSION ENGINE.

China installed 354,000 industrial robots in 2025—59% of the global total and more than 9× U.S. installations. Chinese suppliers also captured 55% of their domestic market.

The significance is not the robots themselves. It is the infrastructure they create for Physical AI.

As intelligence moves from software into machines, China already possesses the manufacturing base, installed equipment and deployment scale onto which AI can be layered.

The Great Split

UNITED STATES

Frontier models → advanced silicon → hyperscale compute → higher-value intelligence

CHINA

Efficient AI → robots → factories → industrial scale → mass diffusion

The strategic question is shifting from:

Who builds the best model?

to:

WHO DEPLOYS INTELLIGENCE INTO THE PHYSICAL ECONOMY FASTEST?

China’s manufacturing scale becomes an AI advantage precisely as machine vision, cheaper inference and more capable models expand the economic use cases for robotics.

Why It Matters for the AI Supercycle

Physical AI expands the Supercycle beyond data centers:

Better models → cheaper inference → smarter machines → more deployment → more edge compute + sensors + memory + networking + power.

The AI infrastructure opportunity therefore broadens rather than contracts.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. America still leads the frontier. Advanced models and compute remain the core U.S. advantage.

2. China increasingly leads deployment scale. 59% of global robot installations is industrial infrastructure, not experimentation.

3. Physical AI is the next battleground. China’s manufacturing installed base creates a powerful deployment advantage.

4. The investment perimeter expands. AI increasingly means not only GPUs and data centers, but robotics, sensors, edge compute, networking, storage and electricity.

THE VELOCITY INSIGHT

America is raising the capability ceiling.

China is scaling the deployment floor.

The next phase will increasingly be decided by:

INTELLIGENCE × MACHINES × DEPLOYMENT VELOCITY

The frontier creates intelligence. Physical AI turns it into industrial power.
