20 September 2026 | FDK
THE TERMS OF POWER
AI, rare earths, energy, capital and regulation are converging into one strategic system. The next phase of The Great Split will be determined less by who owns the best technology than by who controls the terms under which intelligence is built, financed, powered and deployed.
THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION
Who sets the terms of access to the full AI stack?
The race has moved through five stages:
Best Model
→ Best Compute
→ Best Infrastructure
→ Best Capital
→ Best Terms of Access
That final step is now becoming decisive.
⸻
FIVE SIGNALS
1. AI is now part of U.S.–China economic diplomacy
Scott Bessent and He Lifeng meet today ahead of the Trump–Xi summit on 24 September, with AI, rare earths, tariffs and critical minerals on the agenda.
The contest is shifting from pure technological decoupling toward negotiated technological interdependence.
⸻
2. China and the West are running different capital regimes
The Fed, ECB and BOJ have tightened.
China has kept benchmark lending rates unchanged.
The contrast is becoming structural:
Market-driven capital formation
vs.
State-directed industrial capital formation.
Different systems. Same objective:
Control the physical stack of AI.
⸻
3. Europe’s AI opportunity is real—but conditional
The IMF estimates AI could add roughly 1% to European productivity over five years, while around 60% of workers in advanced European economies are highly exposed to AI.
The implication is clear:
Europe’s AI problem is not just technology.
It is:
Energy + Capital + Labor + Single Market + Infrastructure.
Without integration, Europe risks adopting AI while exporting much of the economic rent.
⸻
4. AI is moving into the physical world
Anthropic has established a physical biology laboratory and is exploring how Claude can interact with automated experimental systems under human oversight.
The progression is accelerating:
Text
→ Code
→ Agents
→ Robotics
→ Physical Experiments
→ New Knowledge
This is the beginning of Industrial AI.
⸻
5. Energy security is becoming defense policy
The Middle East continues to show that energy security is no longer simply about production.
It is:
**Production
Routes
Pipelines
Ports
Storage
Military Protection**
The barrel is fungible.
The infrastructure delivering it is not.
⸻
THE INVESTOR TAKEAWAY
The strongest scarcity rents are moving away from generic AI exposure and toward the assets AI cannot function without:
Compute.
Memory.
Optics.
Power.
Grid infrastructure.
Critical minerals.
Permitted capacity.
Physical AI.
The question is no longer:
Who benefits from AI?
It is:
WHO OWNS THE BOTTLENECK—AND CAN CHANGE THE TERMS?

Who has the best model?
It is now moving toward a much larger one:
WHO CONTROLS THE TERMS UNDER WHICH THE ENTIRE SYSTEM OPERATES?
The winners of the Next Economy will increasingly control not only intelligence, but the interfaces around it:
Energy.
Capital.
Infrastructure.

Power belongs to whoever sets the terms.
