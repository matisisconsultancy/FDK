The Price of Proof

Trump–Xi lowered geopolitical risk — it did not lower the hurdle rate, and at 5.2% money AI must now prove demand can clear the cost of capital.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 25, 2026 2 min read Capital
5.23%U.S. 10-year Treasury yield touched
$11.6BAnthropic's seven-year Akamai commitment
2% → 5.2%From buying the promise to demanding proof

“At 5.2%, belief is expensive. Proof is the new premium.”

Trump–Xi lowered geopolitical risk. It did not lower the hurdle rate.

The Washington meeting points toward managed competition, not convergence. The U.S. and China have more room to contain escalation, while the structural contest over AI, semiconductors, critical minerals and industrial capacity continues.

Meanwhile, the U.S. 10-year Treasury touched 5.23%. That is tonight's decisive signal: AI no longer needs to prove that demand exists. It needs to prove that demand can generate returns above the cost of capital.

PROOF IS THE NEW PREMIUM

At 2% money, markets bought the promise. At 5.2%, they demand proof — capital is not leaving AI, it is moving toward bankable AI.

01

Five Signals

what the tape is telling us

  1. At 5.23%, distant cash flows lose value. Time-to-cash now matters as much as growth. signal: The discount rate has become the story.

  2. U.S. orders for computers and communications equipment remain exceptionally strong. The Supercycle is becoming physical. signal: Demand is real and turning into steel and silicon.

  3. Anthropic's $11.6 billion, seven-year Akamai commitment captures the new model: CONTRACT → CAPACITY → UTILIZATION → CASH FLOW. signal: Underwriting beats storytelling.

  4. Washington reduces near-term escalation risk. But two increasingly complete AI-industrial stacks continue to emerge. signal: Détente on the surface, bifurcation underneath.

  5. Capital is not leaving AI. It is moving toward bankable AI. signal: Selectivity is the regime.

Key Takeaways

scale alone no longer wins

  • Investors: favor contracted demand, secured power and visible cash flow.
  • CEOs: measure CAPEX-to-Cash Velocity.
  • Europe: build Industrial AI around its physical economy.
  • Markets: watch the 10-year Treasury, AI credit spreads and energized capacity.
  • The regime: scale alone no longer wins. Bankable scale does.
