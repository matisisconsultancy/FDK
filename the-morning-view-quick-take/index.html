The Morning View: Quick Take

AI, rare earths, energy, capital and regulation are converging into one strategic system — and the next phase of The Great Split will be decided by who controls the terms of access, not who owns the best technology.

“Power belongs to whoever sets the terms.”

The race has moved through five stages — best model, best compute, best infrastructure, best capital, and now best terms of access. That final step is becoming decisive.

The fundamental question is no longer who owns the best technology. It is who sets the terms under which intelligence is built, financed, powered and deployed.

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

Who sets the terms of access to the full AI stack?

01

Five Signals

the convergence taking shape

  1. Scott Bessent and He Lifeng meet today ahead of the Trump–Xi summit on 24 September, with AI, rare earths, tariffs and critical minerals on the agenda. The contest is shifting from pure technological decoupling toward negotiated technological interdependence. signal: Decoupling is giving way to negotiated interdependence.

  2. The Fed, ECB and BOJ have tightened; China has kept benchmark lending rates unchanged. The contrast is becoming structural — market-driven capital formation versus state-directed industrial capital formation. Different systems, same objective: control the physical stack of AI. signal: Two capital regimes, one target — the physical stack.

  3. The IMF estimates AI could add roughly 1% to European productivity over five years, while around 60% of workers in advanced European economies are highly exposed to AI. Europe's problem is not just technology — it is energy, capital, labor, single market and infrastructure. Without integration, Europe risks adopting AI while exporting much of the economic rent. signal: Without integration, Europe adopts AI but exports the rent.

  4. Anthropic has established a physical biology laboratory and is exploring how Claude can interact with automated experimental systems under human oversight. The progression accelerates: text → code → agents → robotics → physical experiments → new knowledge. This is the beginning of Industrial AI. signal: The stack is crossing from text into the physical world.

  5. The Middle East continues to show that energy security is no longer simply about production. It is production, routes, pipelines, ports, storage and military protection. The barrel is fungible; the infrastructure delivering it is not. signal: The barrel is fungible — the infrastructure is not.

Key takeaways

the velocity insight

  • The AI race began with the best model — it is now about who controls the terms under which the entire system operates.
  • Winners will control not only intelligence, but the interfaces around it: energy, capital, infrastructure, critical materials, permission and distribution.
  • Technology creates capability; capital creates scale; infrastructure creates continuity.
