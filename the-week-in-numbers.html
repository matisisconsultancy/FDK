The Week in Numbers.

One thesis ran through every note this week — capital is rotating toward the Infrastructure of Intelligence. Here are the indicators that prove it.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann July 8, 2026 4 min read Recap · Capital · Macro

“The market is still pricing AI as a technology story. It has yet to price it as a capital-allocation revolution.”

From Beyond the AI Trade to The Next Security Order, the week’s field notes told one story from many angles: the value of artificial intelligence is migrating from models to the infrastructure that makes intelligence possible — and capital is already moving. Below, the indicators worth carrying into next week.

01

The capital indicators

The figures the week kept returning to.

Investment & capital formation

The numbers behind the rotation

  • $650B — global AI-infrastructure investment expected in 2026.
  • $300B+ — hyperscaler spend on accelerated computing, AI factories and data centers.
  • $1.3T — South Korea’s public-private strategy over the next decade for chips, AI and advanced manufacturing.
  • €750–800B — additional annual investment the EU estimates it needs to close its competitiveness gap.
  • €500B — Germany’s infrastructure-investment mobilisation.
  • €200B — the EU’s InvestAI initiative.
  • €300B — German labour-shortage cost that accelerated AI deployment could help offset.
  • ~$1T — the next pool of enterprise value, to be captured by owners of intelligence infrastructure.
02

The scenarios that matter

From The Next Security Order — how the geopolitical shock could reprice capital.

45%

Managed Escalation

Limited confrontation, higher risk premium, volatile oil. Capital keeps rotating toward defense, AI infrastructure, energy security and critical logistics.

35%

Hormuz Shock

Shipping disruption, surging insurance costs, sharply higher energy prices and returning inflation. Markets rapidly reprice geopolitical risk.

20%

Strategic Realignment

The conflict widens, Russia and China exploit the vacuum, and markets begin pricing a structurally more fragmented world.

03

The through-line

Four shifts that recurred all week.

01

Build → Deploy

The first supercycle built intelligence; the second industrialises it. Value is moving from models to deployment.

02

Own, don’t rent

Ownership of critical infrastructure — not usage of AI — is becoming the new source of alpha. Landlords, not tenants.

03

Infrastructure = Technology

Electricity, semiconductors, grids, fiber, satellites, defense and mobility have become one asset class: the Infrastructure of Intelligence.

04

Velocity > Scale

Advantage now comes from deploying capital, compute and industrial capacity faster than competitors — not from size alone.
