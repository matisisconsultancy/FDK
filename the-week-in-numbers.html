02 — The scenarios that matter

How the geopolitical shock could reprice capital

From The Next Security Order — three paths, one probability distribution.

45%Managed Escalation
35%Hormuz Shock
20%Strategic Realignment

Managed Escalation

Limited confrontation, higher risk premium, volatile oil. Capital keeps rotating toward defense, AI infrastructure, energy security and critical logistics.

Hormuz Shock

Shipping disruption, surging insurance costs, sharply higher energy prices and returning inflation. Markets rapidly reprice geopolitical risk.

Strategic Realignment

The conflict widens, Russia and China exploit the vacuum, and markets begin pricing a structurally more fragmented world.

03 — The through-line

Four shifts that recurred all week

Build → Deploy

The first supercycle built intelligence; the second industrialises it.

Own ≠ Rent

Ownership of critical infrastructure — not usage of AI — is the new source of alpha.

Infra = Tech

Electricity, chips, grids, fiber, satellites, defense and mobility: one asset class.

Velocity > Scale

Advantage comes from deploying faster than competitors, not from size alone.
