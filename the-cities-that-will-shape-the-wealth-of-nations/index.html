The Cities That Will Shape the Wealth of Nations

Why the global race for intelligence will increasingly be decided by cities — and how the European AI-Native Constellation could redefine the continent's competitive advantage and the future allocation of capital.

Anthropic's reported expansion in Zurich is a signal of a far more consequential transformation: the geographical redistribution of intellectual capital in the AI Supercycle.

What if the next generation of economic superpowers is determined not by the size of national economies, but by the capacity of cities to attract, accumulate, connect and compound intelligence?

The geography of intelligence is becoming a leading indicator of the future geography of wealth.

01

Why This Time Is Different

Artificial intelligence is not simply the outcome of innovation. It is increasingly an input into the production of subsequent innovation. Cities that accumulate intelligence can accelerate the production of further intelligence, generating self-reinforcing cycles of productivity, innovation and capital formation.

Three discontinuities distinguish this cycle: the unprecedented speed at which intelligence deploys across industries; the recursive relationship between intelligence accumulation and innovation; and the ability of small, highly specialised ecosystems to exercise global influence. The paradox: intelligence becomes more accessible globally even as its frontier creation becomes more concentrated geographically.

04

Three Intelligence Magnets

create · scale · industrialise

01

Zurich — Frontier Intelligence

Exceptional research, scientific excellence and intellectual-capital density. Anthropic's reported expansion shows how density can matter more than demographic or geographic scale. Zurich creates intelligence.

02

Madrid — AI Capital

The convergence of energy, accelerated computing, infrastructure investment and financial capital. With Zaragoza and Barcelona it could form an Iberian Intelligence Corridor. Madrid scales intelligence.

03

Milan — Industrial Intelligence

The intersection of financial capital, advanced manufacturing, engineering, design and physical AI, linking Bergamo, Brescia, Turin and Bologna. Milan industrialises intelligence.

The Constellation is the architecture. The Constellation Effect is the economic multiplier.

05

The Constellation Effect

The incremental value interconnected ecosystems generate beyond standalone capability operates through eight reinforcing mechanisms: talent circulation, knowledge recombination, capital mobilisation, infrastructure scale, industrial productivity, market expansion, economic resilience and wealth compounding.

The US has shown the power of concentrated innovation; China, the power of coordinated industrial scaling. Europe has the opportunity to demonstrate the power of interconnected metropolitan intelligence.

06

The Great Repricing of Cities

AI-native cities could trigger a structural geographical reallocation of capital: first-order effects in talent, capex, computing, energy and real estate; second-order effects in productivity, earnings and private markets; third-order effects in valuation multiples, institutional capital rotation, regional fiscal capacity and national competitive advantage.

The highest-ranked cities will not necessarily deliver the highest returns. The greatest opportunities may emerge where accelerating intelligence accumulation and network integration are not yet reflected in asset valuations — across industrial equities, energy infrastructure, accelerated computing, high-speed rail, real estate, growth equity and infrastructure debt.

Key Takeaways For Investors

the next geographical rotation

  • Anticipate the rotation by identifying cities where intelligence accumulates faster than valuations.
  • Look beyond technology equities toward energy, computing, industrial AI, high-speed rail and real estate.
  • Incorporate the Constellation Effect into long-term geographical allocation as evidence matures.
  • Prioritise ecosystems that translate intellectual capital into earnings, free cash flow and returns.
  • Identify emerging Intelligence Magnets before their advantages are priced into market multiples.

Cities will create intelligence. Constellations will compound wealth. Nations will rise or fall on their ability to connect the two.
