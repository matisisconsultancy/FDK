The Orchestration Premium

Intelligence is now abundant enough that the decisive advantage is the ability to orchestrate intelligence, networks, capital and physical assets without leaving any of them idle.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 18, 2026 7 min read Capital

“The next corporate advantage is not owning the largest system — it is making the whole system work together faster.”

Anthropic now says Claude leads 26% of the work required to build its next generation of AI models. Marvell and GlobalFoundries expand optical capacity because processors are increasingly waiting for data. Bolt and Lucid move autonomous mobility from pilots toward a 25,000-vehicle European fleet. CoreWeave raises another $3 billion as contracted power reaches 4.2 GW.

The Next Economy is crossing another threshold: the decisive advantage is increasingly the ability to orchestrate intelligence, networks, capital and physical assets without leaving any of them idle. Four developments. One system. The competitive bottleneck is migrating from intelligence itself toward the orchestration of everything intelligence requires.

The macro environment remains demanding but less hostile than 48 hours ago. U.S. equities rebounded sharply on September 17 as oil and Treasury yields eased. Yet the global cost of capital remains structurally higher: the Bank of Japan raised its policy rate today to 1.25%, the highest in 31 years, following this week's Federal Reserve increase.

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

What if the corporation shaping the Next Economy fastest is no longer the one owning the best component — but the one capable of coordinating specialized chips, autonomous agents, networks, electricity, capital and physical machines into one productive system?

01

The Decisive Corporate Inflections

four developments, one system

  1. Anthropic disclosed on September 17 that Claude now leads 26% of its own AI R&D work, up from only 1% in March, and collaborates with humans on more than 90% of the research workflow. Roughly 30,000 agents were performing research and engineering work simultaneously in August. Every proposed action is screened before execution — among more than one billion decisions during the month, approximately one in 47,000 was blocked. Around 6% of research compute in one sampled week went to safety work, rising to 12% for research conducted by AI systems themselves.

    The new architecture is: human researchers → AI collaborators → AI-led workstreams → next-generation AI. This is the first credible corporate expression of recursive productivity acceleration. But the most important number may be the blocked-action rate: scale without control would destroy institutional deployability. The next frontier is not autonomous AI — it is governable autonomy. signal: AI is beginning to participate materially in the production of the next generation of AI.

  2. Marvell and GlobalFoundries expanded a multi-year agreement to increase manufacturing of silicon-germanium technology at GlobalFoundries' Vermont facility — chips used in pluggable optical transceivers, near-packaged and co-packaged optics. Both explicitly linked the added capacity to rapidly growing data movement inside AI data centers.

    The first AI bottleneck was compute, then memory, then power. Now system economics collide with data movement. An expensive accelerator running at 70% utilization because data cannot reach it fast enough is not a semiconductor problem — it is a capital-productivity problem created by the network. signal: The Next Economy rewards the semiconductor solving the system bottleneck, not necessarily the one with the smallest transistor.

  3. Bolt and Lucid plan to deploy at least 25,000 fully autonomous vehicles across Europe, with Bolt targeting 100,000 on its platform by 2035. Bolt will own and operate the fleet; the cars will use Nvidia's Hyperion system and be manufactured at Lucid's new Saudi facility, expected to begin operating next year.

    The unit of analysis changes from "does the technology work?" to "can the fleet economics work?" A robotaxi platform must coordinate manufacturing, autonomy hardware, software, charging, maintenance, dispatch, insurance, regulation and utilization. Autonomous driving is now an operating-system problem. signal: Physical AI will be won by whoever converts autonomy into asset utilization — not whoever demonstrates it first.

  4. CoreWeave launched a $3 billion convertible-debt offering with a $500 million buyer option, plus a program to sell up to 35 million shares (around $2.92 billion at Wednesday's close). Contracted power rose to about 4.2 GW from 3.7 GW at June-end, while some recent short-duration compute contracts priced near $40 million per megawatt annualized. The company previously disclosed a $104.2 billion backlog and over $25 billion of additional third-quarter commitments.

    The chain is customer demand → contracted power → debt/equity → compute → utilization → cash conversion. The first four arrows are increasingly proven; the fifth remains decisive. Capital formation is not the same thing as capital productivity — a distinction that keeps CoreWeave outside the Top 10 despite exceptional growth. signal: A company able to raise billions repeatedly possesses financing Velocity — but every dollar raises the hurdle for economic conversion.

Hidden Patterns

where value is migrating

Intelligence Becomes Recursive

AI once increased human productivity; now it increasingly increases AI-development productivity. If the loop compounds, conventional assumptions about innovation cycles become obsolete — but the same recursion magnifies control risk.

02

Optics Is Capital Productivity

A trillion-dollar installed compute base earns poor returns if accelerators wait for information. Faster interconnect increases processor utilization, energy productivity and capital productivity — moving optical bandwidth to the center of the AI production function.

03

Mature Nodes Capture Frontier Value

GlobalFoundries does not compete with TSMC at 2nm, yet becomes more strategic because AI needs silicon germanium, power management, photonics, analog and sensors. The supply chain is broadening faster than the transistor is shrinking.

04

Physical AI Changes the Unit of Competition

Digital AI scales by adding software users; Physical AI must coordinate physical assets — hardware × regulation × fleet operations × utilization × maintenance. Higher barriers, but enormous value for whoever solves the whole equation.

05

The Capital Market Turns Continuous

Debt, convertibles, at-the-market equity, private credit, customer prepayments and project finance now form the AI factory's own capital ecosystem. Capital availability remains enormous; cheap capital does not — a distinction sharpened by the Fed and a 31-year-high BoJ rate.

06

Community Permission Is Infrastructure

Silicon Valley faces growing resistance to data-center projects over electricity, water and pollution; California lawmakers approved transparency and cost-sharing measures. An approved megawatt is worth more than a requested one.

Key takeaways

from scale to synchronization

  • 26% of Anthropic's AI R&D is now led by Claude, up from 1% in March; ~30,000 agents ran concurrently in August with about 1 in 47,000 actions blocked.
  • Marvell (+1.0) and GlobalFoundries (+0.8) mark optical bandwidth as a binding AI system bottleneck alongside power and memory.
  • Bolt (+0.9) and Lucid (+0.9) move autonomous mobility toward 25,000 European vehicles now and 100,000 on-platform by 2035.
  • CoreWeave's $3B raise, 4.2 GW contracted power and $104.2B backlog prove demand — but capital productivity, not capital formation, is the decisive test.
  • The premium is migrating toward businesses with scarce assets, high utilization, multiple paths around bottlenecks and contractual demand.
