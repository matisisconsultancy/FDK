The Rise of Velocity

Competing in the Age of Agentic AI

by Francesco de Leo Kaufmann, Ph.D.

DeepSeek collapsed AI costs by 95% overnight. Sweden pivoted from industrial power to AI sovereign in months. Microsoft's trillion-dollar empire now turns on millisecond decisions. The Rise of Velocity argues that we are witnessing the end of the scale economy and the birth of the velocity economy — where advantage is no longer measured in years but in learning cycles. Through fourteen frameworks, drawn from Silicon Valley to Beijing and from Europe's regulatory battles to the Gulf's sovereign-AI strategies, it reveals how agentic AI has rewritten the mechanics of global power. The future is being written at machine speed; those still planning at human pace have already lost.

From the opening — The Bottom Line

We are witnessing the emergence of Velocity Capitalism — a new economic paradigm where speed, not scale, determines competitive advantage.

At 3:47 a.m. Pacific Time on January 20, 2025, something unprecedented happened. DeepSeek, a Chinese startup virtually unknown outside specialist circles, released an open-source model that matched OpenAI's GPT-4 performance. The shocking part wasn't the capability — it was the cost. While OpenAI had spent over $100 million training GPT-4, DeepSeek achieved comparable results for under $6 million.

Within 48 hours, DeepSeek's app topped Apple's App Store. Within a week, it triggered $1 trillion in market-value destruction across U.S. tech giants. The comfortable assumptions of Silicon Valley — that AI dominance required massive capital, that moats could be built with money — evaporated overnight.

This wasn't just a technical disruption. It was a velocity disruption.

Inside the book

Six parts, fourteen frameworks — from the architecture of velocity to the sovereign-AI race, the complete blueprint for competing when intelligence becomes autonomous and acceleration becomes survival.

  1. 01

    The Velocity Paradigm

    How speed, not scale, became the decisive factor in competition. The architectural foundations of accelerated computing — and why the advantages of the industrial age are evaporating in quarters, not decades.

  2. 02

    Economic Transformation

    How velocity reshapes markets — driving extreme concentration and demanding new metrics for value. The industrial organisation of AI infrastructure, and the great repricing of global assets already underway.

  3. 03

    Models and Applications

    The strategic tensions between AI architectures — large versus small models, proprietary versus open source, the rise of AI super-apps — and what each fault line means for competitive advantage.

  4. 04

    The Agentic Evolution

    How embedded agents are rewriting the logic of work itself — transforming organisations from the inside out, and opening the new code wars over technological supremacy.

  5. 05

    Strategic Deployments

    Velocity in action — from Apple's edge intelligence and the xAI–X merger to the rise of humanoid robots as an entirely new trillion-dollar asset class.

  6. 06

    Global Competition

    How nations position themselves in the velocity race — from Europe's regulatory approach to Sweden's sovereign-AI model and the Wallenberg–NVIDIA blueprint for national AI independence.
