The Rise of Velocity

How speed became the only moat that matters

by Francesco de Leo Kaufmann

Speed has quietly become the only durable moat. This volume traces how velocity — of learning, of decision, of deployment — replaced scale as the decisive variable, and how the fastest organisations compound advantages the slow can no longer catch.

From the opening chapter

Speed is the last moat standing.

Every classic source of advantage — scale, distribution, brand, even data — can now be rented, replicated or routed around. What cannot be copied is the rate at which an organisation learns and acts.

Velocity compounds. A company that decides and deploys twice as fast does not merely move quicker; it runs more experiments, captures more feedback and pulls away on a curve the slow can never close.
