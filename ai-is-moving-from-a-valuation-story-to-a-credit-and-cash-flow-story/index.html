AI Is Moving From a Valuation Story to a Credit-and-cash-flow Story.

Three signals now line up — and they suggest AI is already reshaping financial conditions before its full productivity dividend is visible.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 30, 2026 2 min read Capital

“The next rotation begins when capital stops rewarding who builds the most AI.”

Three signals now line up: the Fed is increasingly focused on higher real yields and the financing intensity of the technology investment cycle; the ECB sees AI pressure across semiconductor capacity, investment and corporate financing; and financial supervisors are increasingly examining concentration, leverage and interconnectedness around the AI ecosystem.

The implication is direct. AI is already reshaping financial conditions before its full productivity dividend is visible. Two second-order effects now matter most.

THE HIDDEN TRANSMISSION CHANNEL

The next AI correction could move from equities into credit — and from credit into real investment.

01

The Second-Order Effects

what to watch now

  1. The AI Supercycle is increasingly financed through a much broader ecosystem: data centres, power, grids, project finance, private credit and leveraged suppliers. The key question is no longer simply whether AI demand continues. It is whether the cash flows can clear the new cost of capital.

    If expected returns weaken while real yields remain high, the chain runs from refinancing pressure to wider spreads, to capex cuts, to project cancellations, to asset repricing. signal: Stress-test refinancing beneath the AI build-out.

  2. The first phase rewarded those selling the infrastructure. The next phase should increasingly reward those using AI to transform their economics. The hierarchy shifts from AI narrative, to AI capex, to AI adoption, to productivity, to margin expansion, to free cash flow.

    Higher discount rates accelerate that transition because distant promises become less valuable. The next leadership group should increasingly be companies already demonstrating lower unit costs, higher output per worker, margin expansion, higher returns on capital and visible free cash flow. signal: The new divide is not AI vs non-AI — it is AI capital consumers vs AI economic captors.

02

The Transmission Chains

how stress propagates

01

Credit chain

Refinancing pressure → wider spreads → capex cuts → project cancellations → asset repricing.

02

Value chain

AI narrative → AI capex → AI adoption → productivity → margin expansion → free cash flow.

03

AI capital consumers

Businesses whose spend funds the build-out but whose returns must still clear a higher cost of capital.

04

AI economic captors

Businesses already proving AI raises margins, returns and free cash flow.

Investor verdict

watch credit first, follow cash flow second

  • Watch credit first — the build-out is the fault line.
  • Follow cash flow second — reward economic capture, not capex.
  • Stress-test refinancing beneath the AI build-out.
  • Shift equity exposure toward businesses proving that AI raises margins and returns rather than merely CapEx.
