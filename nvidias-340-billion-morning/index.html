Nvidia’s $340 Billion Morning

Yesterday's earnings were extraordinary; this morning's $340 billion move is the real story — markets are no longer valuing a chipmaker, they are repricing the duration of the AI Supercycle.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann August 27, 2026 9 min read Capital
$96.2BQ2 FY2027 revenue, up 106% year-on-year and 18% sequentially
$89.0BData Center revenue, up 117% year-on-year — nearly 93% of total
$108BNext-quarter guidance (±2%), assuming no China Data Center revenue
+$340BIndicated market-cap gain on a 6.7% pre-market move to $223.71

“"Now, compute is revenue." — Jensen Huang”

The market is no longer valuing a semiconductor company. It is repricing the expected duration of the AI Supercycle.

NVIDIA reported extraordinary numbers yesterday. But the most important number arrived this morning. In pre-market trading, NVIDIA shares jumped 6.7% to $223.71, putting the company on course to add approximately $340 billion of market capitalization in a single trading session.

A company already worth more than $5 trillion can produce another $340 billion of expected value overnight—not because it discovered a new product category yesterday, but because investors revised upward their assumptions about the duration, scale and monetization of the global AI investment cycle. That is the real story of NVIDIA's results.

Yesterday was not simply another earnings beat. It was a duration shock. The market is beginning to price the possibility that the AI Supercycle is longer, broader and economically more productive than previously assumed. And that changes the equation for the entire Next Economy.

03

Five Signals

what the print revealed

  1. NVIDIA's revenue has more than doubled in twelve months. signal: The revenue base itself has doubled in a single year.

  2. AI infrastructure is overwhelmingly driving the economic machine. signal: Data Center is now the company — nearly 93% of revenue.

  3. Growth is continuing from an already extraordinary base—and the guidance assumes no Data Center computing revenue from China. signal: Guidance climbs even after zeroing out China.

  4. The market received evidence that the Supercycle could have considerably greater duration than feared. signal: Duration, not just magnitude, is being confirmed.

  5. Capital markets are not simply rewarding another earnings beat. They are extending the expected duration of the AI investment cycle. signal: The move prices tomorrow's duration, not yesterday's beat.

Five Implications For Investors, CEOs And Boards

the wider field

  • NVIDIA is becoming a macro variable — its results increasingly provide a quarterly reading on global AI capital formation.
  • The AI trade is expanding outward, to include power, grids, data centers, memory, networking, cooling and industrial automation.
  • The next valuation test will be free cash flow — markets will separate companies that merely spend on AI from those capable of monetizing it.
  • Physical AI may become the next frontier, extending intelligence toward robotics, autonomous systems, factories, mobility and industrial processes.
  • The Great Split will widen — those combining accelerated computing, energy, capital and industrial execution will move faster than those that cannot.
