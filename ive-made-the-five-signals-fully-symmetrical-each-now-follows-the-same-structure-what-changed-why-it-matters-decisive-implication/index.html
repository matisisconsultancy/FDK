I’ve Made the Five Signals Fully Symmetrical: Each Now Follows the Same Structure: What Changed → Why It Matters → Decisive Implication.

The constraint on the Next Economy is no longer technology alone — it is the ability to connect compute, energy, capital, regulation and demand fast enough to turn innovation into cash flow.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 20, 2026 4 min read Macro
~5%U.S. 10-year Treasury yield resetting hurdle rates
>$100Brent crude, with Hormuz traffic still impaired
>50%higher gross dies per wafer on China's newest DRAM platform

“Coordinated velocity beats fragmented scale.”

The U.S. 10-year is near 5%, Brent remains above $100, Hormuz traffic is still impaired, Washington and Beijing are negotiating AI guardrails and critical minerals, and China's newest DRAM platform claims more than 50% higher gross dies per wafer. The constraint on the Next Economy is no longer technology alone. It is the ability to connect compute, energy, capital, regulation and demand fast enough to convert innovation into economic output.

The next phase will not necessarily reward those with the most GPUs, capital or industrial scale. It will reward those that can turn technological acceleration into cash flow before infrastructure, financing or policy become binding constraints.

The fundamental question: who can convert technological acceleration into economic power before the surrounding system slows it down?

INVESTOR VERDICT

Own the control points; underweight the assets that pay for scarcity, wait for permission or refinance at 5% money.

01

The Investor Action Box

own bottlenecks, avoid victims

01

Long / Accumulate

Semiconductors, HBM and memory — structural scarcity and sovereign demand. Optical networking and interconnect, as larger compute clusters require more bandwidth. Contracted data centres with secured power, utilisation and long-duration customers. Grid equipment, nuclear, gas and storage. AI security and evaluation. Banks, insurers and infrastructure-finance platforms.

02

Underweight

Uncontracted AI capacity — gigawatts without committed demand are expensive optionality. Legacy industrials with weak pricing power. Highly leveraged long-duration assets, as 5% sovereign yields reset hurdle rates. Narrative technology, where cash conversion matters more than multiple expansion.

03

Avoid

Stranded scale: no secured power, no contracted demand, no regulatory certainty and no credible return above WACC.

04

The Portfolio Test

Does the asset control a bottleneck — or pay for one?

02

Five Signals

from capability to coordination

  1. Washington and Beijing are discussing AI behaviour, critical minerals, tariffs and energy together. AI has moved beyond the technology stack and into geopolitical architecture. signal: The competitive edge increasingly comes from shaping the rules that govern strategic systems.

  2. AI is increasing industrial productivity while domestic demand remains comparatively weak. Higher productivity can amplify overcapacity rather than solve it: more productivity → more output → lower unit costs → more export pressure → more trade friction. signal: China's next constraint is not production. It is demand absorption.

  3. China's domestic memory industry is improving yield, density and manufacturing economics. Producing a chip is not enough; sovereignty becomes durable only when production is competitive at scale. signal: The decisive metric moves from technological feasibility to cost, yield and volume economics.

  4. Funding is broadening from hyperscalers toward banks, sovereign funds, private credit and insurers. AI infrastructure is beginning to resemble a financeable asset class: contracted capacity → predictable cash flow → collateral → lower cost of capital → more scale. signal: The underwriting test is now whether capacity can generate infrastructure-like returns.

  5. Hormuz remains impaired and oil remains above $100 while energy increasingly enters trade and strategic negotiations. Energy scarcity now transmits directly into inflation, rates, WACC and industrial competitiveness. signal: Energy security is becoming a determinant of economic velocity.

Key takeaways

the coordination premium

  • Own the control points; avoid the bottleneck victims.
  • Scale provides resources; capital provides optionality; technology provides capability — but execution determines whether they compound.
  • At 5% money, the market rewards cash flow, contracts and control over narrative.
  • The new Great Split is coordinated velocity versus fragmented scale.

That is today's Velocity Advantage.
