The 136 and 144 Action Triggers-in Focus

Neither 136 nor 144 should be traded mechanically — a threshold without confirmation is observation, a threshold with confirmation is information.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann October 2, 2026 4 min read Macro

“The number opens the door. Confirmation tells us whether to walk through it.”

The Spread Level opens the test. Cross-Market Confirmation activates the signal. Neither 136 nor 144 should be traded mechanically.

The framework is a single sequence: LEVEL → CONFIRMATION → ACTION. A threshold without confirmation is observation. A threshold with confirmation is information.

THE SEQUENCE

136 asks whether to stop treating this as merely European. 144 asks whether to stop treating this as merely sovereign. That distinction makes the framework tradable without making it mechanical.

01

The Trigger Levels

level, confirmation, action

  1. A BTP–Bund move through 136 bps opens the Italy test. It does not complete it. The required confirmation is that at least one primary Italian cross-spread widens — BTP–Bonos wider, and preferably BTP–OAT wider — with the other not contradicting the signal.

    The strongest configuration is BTP–Bund ≥ 136, BTP–Bonos ↑, BTP–OAT ↑, and Italian banks underperforming European banks. If confirmed, reclassify the move from European stress to Italian differentiation: the market is now attaching an incremental risk premium specifically to Italy.

    The question changes from How much is Europe repricing? to How much additional financing disadvantage is Italy beginning to acquire versus Europe?

    Signal 136 alone = WATCH. 136 + wider cross-spreads = ITALY SIGNAL. 136 + cross-spreads + bank underperformance = STRONG ITALY SIGNAL.

  2. Do not activate the Italy signal if BTP–Bund crosses 136 while OAT–Bund ↑, Bonos–Bund ↑, and both BTP–Bonos and BTP–OAT remain broadly stable. The move remains predominantly European.

    If BTP–Bund subsequently moves back below the threshold and Italian cross-spreads retrace, the differentiation signal weakens. Do not treat an intraday touch as a structural break. Persistence matters.

    Signal An intraday touch is not a break — require persistence.

  3. A move toward or through 144 bps opens a different test. By this point, Italian differentiation should already be established; if it has not, 144 cannot be treated as an Italian transmission signal. The prerequisite is that BTP–Bonos and/or BTP–OAT are already wider. Italy must first separate from Europe. Only then can we test whether that separation is transmitting.

    Now stop looking primarily at sovereign cross-spreads and move down the financing chain: Italian banks vs. European banks, Italian bank credit vs. European bank credit, Italian corporate credit vs. European corporate credit.

    If confirmed, reclassify the move from Italian sovereign differentiation to Italian financial transmission. The question becomes: What Italian assets and projects will now face a higher cost of capital?

    Signal 144 alone = WATCH. 144 + differentiation = TRANSMISSION TEST ACTIVE. 144 + bank underperformance = EARLY TRANSMISSION. 144 + bank + credit deterioration = TRANSMISSION CONFIRMED.

  4. Do not call regime change if BTP–Bund reaches 144 but BTP–Bonos and BTP–OAT are stable, Italian banks remain resilient relative to Europe, and Italian credit shows no relative deterioration. The headline spread is wider, but transmission is not confirmed — 144 without downstream confirmation remains a sovereign-market signal.

    If Italian bank and credit underperformance reverses while sovereign cross-spreads tighten, the transmission thesis weakens. The critical distinction is touching 144 versus building a financing regime around 144. The second is what matters.

    Signal Require persistence across markets, not a single intraday print.

03

The Action Matrix

level · confirmation · classification · focus

01

131 — Baseline

Persistence confirms European/sovereign repricing. Investor focus: establish baseline.

02

136 — Italy Differentiation

BTP–Bonos / BTP–OAT wider signals Italy vs. Europe. Focus: Italy relative to Europe.

03

136+ — Strong Italy Signal

Cross-spreads plus weaker banks. Focus: Italian financials.

04

144 — Transmission Test

Italy differentiation intact. Focus: banks and credit.

05

144+ — Transmission Confirmed

Banks and credit underperform. Focus: domestic cost of capital.

06

144 + ~5% BTP — Capital-Formation Stress

Investment-sensitive assets weaken. Focus: AI/infrastructure capex.

Key takeaways

the Monday rule

  • At 136, move from Europe to Italy only if the cross-spreads confirm.
  • At 144, move from Italy sovereigns to the financing system only if banks and credit confirm.
  • Near 5%, move from finance to capital formation only if investment-sensitive assets confirm.
  • A threshold without confirmation is observation; a threshold with confirmation is information.
