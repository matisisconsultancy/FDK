The Thesis Architecture

Velocity Capitalism — The new physics of competition

Speed has replaced scale. The organisations that will lead are not those with the most assets — they are those with the fastest learning cycles. Every board still optimising for quarterly planning is operating on the wrong clock.

Structural Bifurcation — The divergence already underway

Sectors are splitting between organisations that embed intelligence and those that procure it. The gap is quiet today. In 36 months, it will be visible in every valuation, every performance metric, every competitive conversation.

The Repricing — The mispricing that remains

European infrastructure operators are valued as yield vehicles on depreciating assets. The correct framework treats AI-native operators as learning systems whose performance improves with time. Markets have not yet absorbed this. They will.

The Civic Platform — A different capital logic

AI-native infrastructure improves through use rather than depreciating through it. This inverts the standard financial logic — and demands different governance structures, different capital timelines, and a different understanding of what return means.
