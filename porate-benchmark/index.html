Porate Benchmark

The AI Supercycle has entered its capital-allocation phase — the market is moving from AI spending to AI economic spread.

“Follow the cash, not the capex.”

The AI Supercycle has entered its capital-allocation phase. The financial test is no longer whether companies can deploy more AI capital. It is whether that capital can generate cash before financing costs, depreciation and technological obsolescence consume the return.

With the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield at 5.24% at the September 28 close, the hurdle has moved higher. Every dollar committed to compute, data centers, power infrastructure or frontier models now has to convert into utilization and free cash flow fast enough to preserve economic spread over the cost of capital.

At the same time, the return pool is fragmenting. Model capability is proliferating and inference costs are falling, while advanced packaging, power, cooling and grid access remain constrained. Scarcity is therefore migrating toward the physical layers where pricing power is stronger and capacity takes longer to reproduce.

THE FINANCIAL HIERARCHY

Scarcity → Utilization → Pricing Power → Free Cash Flow → ROIC − Cost of Capital. Capex is not the signal. Productive cash conversion is.

03

Two Decisive Structural Drivers

What separates leaders now

  1. The next constraints increasingly sit in advanced packaging → memory → networking → power → cooling → grid access. These layers cannot scale at software speed, creating a transmission mechanism of scarcity → pricing power → utilization → cash conversion → ROIC. TSMC captures manufacturing scarcity, Schneider Electric captures power architecture, Siemens Energy sits against generation and grid constraints, and Vertiv monetizes thermal density. signal: The second-order AI trade is everything compute cannot operate without.

  2. The next divide will not be between companies that invest in AI and those that do not. It will be between capital that compounds — capex → utilization → cash flow → ROIC → reinvestment — and capital that merely accumulates — capex → capacity → depreciation → financing cost → margin pressure. The question is no longer how much is being spent, but how rapidly each incremental dollar becomes productive free cash flow. signal: That is the emerging hurdle rate of the AI Supercycle.

Three Forward Signals

Where monetization is decided

  1. Grid access increasingly determines when capital begins earning. Time-to-power therefore becomes a financial variable — not simply an engineering constraint. signal: When power arrives, capital starts earning.

  2. As intelligence becomes cheaper, control of workflow, customer and transaction becomes more valuable. signal: Cheaper models raise the value of distribution.

  3. Agentic AI creates more identities, permissions, transactions and liabilities. More autonomy drives greater security intensity. signal: Distribution and security determine how scarce capital is monetized.

Key Takeaways

The economic spread, not the spend

  • Physical scarcity is becoming the first great redistribution mechanism of AI economics.
  • The value of AI increasingly moves toward the layers that cannot scale at software speed.
  • Capital productivity — not capex scale — is becoming the decisive financial test.
  • Backlog quality increasingly depends on conversion into powered, utilized, cash-generating capacity.
  • The next market leaders should be those that combine scarce assets with superior capital-to-cash conversion.
