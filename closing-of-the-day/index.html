Closing of the Day

AI is no longer only repricing technology — it is beginning to reprice the physical economy.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann August 17, 2026 6 min read Capital

“As AI moves from the cloud into the physical world, capital will follow intelligence.”

What if the most important signal from today is not where markets closed—but where capital has begun to move?

The answer emerging from August 17 is increasingly clear: the AI Supercycle is entering its capital-intensive phase. For three years, markets largely rewarded the companies producing intelligence: semiconductors, accelerated computing, memory, models and cloud infrastructure. The next phase will increasingly reward the companies capable of deploying intelligence into energy systems, factories, construction sites, transportation networks and machines.

That shift changes the economics of AI—and potentially the architecture of modern capitalism.

The Five Signals

The structure beneath the rotation

  1. The financing requirements are moving from billions toward hundreds of billions. signal: Compute is now a capital-markets project, not just a purchase order.

  2. An 8-GW data center is simultaneously a technology project, an electricity project and a capital-markets project. signal: Power is no longer a footnote to compute — it is the constraint.

  3. Industrial AI reaches physical reality through machines capable of sensing, reasoning and acting. signal: Robots are the execution layer of Industrial AI.

  4. The first winners supplied intelligence. The next winners will increasingly apply intelligence to physical assets. signal: Follow the next dollar of capex beyond semiconductors.

  5. A Reuters poll published today found most economists expecting the Federal Reserve to keep its policy rate at 3.50%–3.75% through year-end. The largest technological capital-expansion cycle in decades is developing inside a structurally more expensive capital regime. signal: That combination will separate winners from losers.

THE THIRD PHASE

The first phase increased the value of companies capable of creating intelligence. The second, companies capable of manufacturing intelligence at scale. The third phase is beginning now — and will reward the companies capable of embedding intelligence into productive physical capital.

Key takeaways

The Great Capital Rotation

  • The AI Supercycle is migrating from a technology valuation story into a capital-formation regime.
  • Financing structures now resemble those of railways, telecom and electricity — equity alone cannot fund the next stage.
  • Robotics is not a separate theme; it is the mechanism through which intelligence acquires physical agency.
  • Once productivity gains finance the next generation of intelligent capital equipment, the cycle becomes self-reinforcing.
  • Do not ask only who owns the best model — ask who finances the infrastructure, supplies the electricity, manufactures the machines and controls the deployment layer.
