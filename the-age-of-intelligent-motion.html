The Age of Intelligent Motion

Mobility reimagined for an AI-native world

by Francesco de Leo Kaufmann

Mobility is the first sector being rebuilt around artificial intelligence. This volume maps how AI-native operators turn every journey into data, every route into a learning loop and every fleet into an asset that improves with use — and what that means for the operators still running on timetables and depreciation schedules.

From the opening chapter

Movement is becoming intelligent.

For a hundred years a transport network was a fixed asset — track, vehicles, depots and a timetable set in advance. Its value decayed from the day it opened.

AI-native mobility inverts that logic. The network learns from every trip; the more it is used, the more accurate, efficient and valuable it becomes. The operators that grasp this are not buying technology — they are changing what an asset is.
