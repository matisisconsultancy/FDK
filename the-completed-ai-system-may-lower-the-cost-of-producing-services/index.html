The completed AI system may lower the cost of producing services.

AI is turning deflationary for headcount while remaining inflationary for capital, electricity and scarce industrial inputs.

“A company can be strategically right and financially overowned.”

During the transition, AI can reduce the labor required to satisfy each additional unit of demand. It is becoming deflationary for headcount while remaining inflationary for capital, electricity and scarce industrial inputs.

This is not a traditional recession signal. Demand is still expanding. It is a signal that the relationship between economic activity and labor absorption is changing.

The Great Split is moving from financial markets into factor incomes: capital receives the productivity upside immediately; labor must renegotiate its role inside the new productive system.

THE SPLIT

01

AI Demand Is Accelerating. Valuation Support Is Not.

The market withdraws unconditional capital

02

The FDK Interpretation

Where industrial reality and financial pricing diverge

  1. The semiconductor selloff does not invalidate the AI infrastructure cycle. It reveals the difference between industrial acceleration, financial expectations, and the price already paid for future leadership.

    The market can believe simultaneously that accelerated-computing demand will remain extraordinary and that individual securities have discounted too much of that future too early. signal: A company can be strategically right and financially overowned.

  2. The AI trade is broadening economically while narrowing financially. More companies are benefiting from data-centre construction, semiconductors, memory, power, networking and enterprise deployment. But fewer companies are receiving unconditional permission to spend, finance and scale without demonstrating incremental margins and cash-flow conversion. signal: Fewer companies now hold unconditional permission to spend.

  3. The next winners will not merely report AI exposure. They will prove that AI revenue is increasing faster than the capital required to generate it. signal: Prove revenue outpaces the capital that generates it.

The Real Terrain

Making intelligence operational inside factories, transportation, energy, defense, healthcare, software and critical infrastructure. Europe may not need to defeat the United States in frontier-model scale to capture a decisive share of AI value. The bottleneck is moving from access to models toward the integration of models with proprietary data, legacy systems, regulation, security and physical operations. This is Europe's natural competitive terrain.

Key takeaways

What the split means for capital

  • AI is deflationary for headcount but inflationary for capital, power and scarce inputs.
  • The semiconductor selloff reflects overowned securities, not a broken infrastructure cycle.
  • Hyperscaler capex nears $800 billion in 2026 as tech-sector earnings rise 72%.
  • Unconditional permission to spend is being withdrawn from companies without margin proof.
  • Europe's edge lies in making intelligence operational inside the real economy.
