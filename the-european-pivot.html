From the opening chapter

The map of advantage is being redrawn.

The conventional story says Europe is where artificial intelligence arrives late — over- regulated, under-capitalised, structurally cautious. That story mistakes constraint for disadvantage.

Europe's density of public infrastructure, its regulatory rigour and its appetite for trust are precisely the conditions under which AI-native operators compound. The pivot is already underway; the markets have simply not priced it yet.
