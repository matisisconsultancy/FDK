Electricity, Infrastructure, Construction and Capital.

The decisive question is not whether AI is inflationary or deflationary — it is which effect dominates at which layer, and why the next Great Split runs between scarcity owners and duration buyers.

FdK Francesco De Leo Kaufmann September 15, 2026 6 min read Capital
100 / 100Morning Velocity Reading — The Scarcity Premium
$90+Brent, with missiles again flying across the Gulf
4.34%U.S. two-year Treasury, as September hike pricing firms
2.7%French harmonized inflation, energy-led

“The price of intelligence falls. The value of what intelligence cannot exist without rises.”

The decisive question is therefore not whether AI is inflationary or deflationary. It is: which effect dominates at which layer and over what period of time? That is the analytical framework central banks, investors and policymakers increasingly need.

This morning gives us a clearer view of where the AI Supercycle is going — not toward one single bottleneck, but toward a sequence of them. Accelerated computing. Memory. Optics. Power. Energy security. Capital. Every time the system solves one scarcity, growth pushes pressure into another.

THE GREAT SPLIT

Not simply AI versus non-AI — but those who own the scarce complements of intelligence versus those who must continuously finance access to them.

Strategic Agenda for Today and the Week Ahead

eight things to watch

  1. The G20 finance ministers and central-bank governors meet today and tomorrow. The agenda has moved beyond conventional macro coordination. Watch whether other countries align with Washington on secondary Iran sanctions and whether Bessent can build a coalition around tougher trade treatment of China. signal: The finance table is now a geopolitical table.

  2. Watch Brent around $90–91. If military escalation continues, the inflation premium will immediately propagate into bonds and rate expectations. If actual Gulf flows hold near the recently improved 15–16 million barrels per day, markets may still distinguish between military escalation and true supply destruction. signal: Escalation and supply destruction are not yet the same price.

  3. Watch the two-year Treasury around 4.34% and September hike pricing. Barclays now expects both September and December hikes. A further move higher in short rates would strengthen the message that Warsh has decisively changed the policy regime. signal: The short end is telling you the regime has changed.

  4. If the yen continues weakening without intervention while Japanese yields rise, markets may conclude that Tokyo prefers monetary adjustment over currency management. That would matter for global capital flows far beyond Japan. signal: Duration pressure in Tokyo is a global signal.

  5. Energy has already pushed French inflation to 2.7%. If German and euro-area readings confirm renewed pressure, the probability of another ECB increase at the September 10 meeting will rise further. signal: Energy is reloading the ECB's decision.

  6. Nvidia has validated extraordinary general-purpose accelerated-computing demand. Broadcom becomes the next major test of customized AI silicon and networking. The company has previously said AI-chip revenue could exceed $100 billion by 2027, while its partnerships with Google and Anthropic extend through multiple generations of custom AI infrastructure. signal: Custom silicon is the next demand test.

  7. On September 2, Enflame begins subscriptions for its roughly $892 million Shanghai AI-chip IPO — a test of appetite for another domestic accelerated-computing player as China's semiconductor push accelerates but valuations grow more disciplined. Meanwhile, Meta plans to put its Iris AI chip into production this month, part of a strategy to push total computing capacity toward 14 gigawatts in 2027 and reduce dependence on external accelerator suppliers. signal: Sovereign and in-house silicon are both scaling at once.

  8. After Warsh moved markets dramatically toward a September hike, the labor data now becomes the principal counterweight to inflation. The September 16 Fed decision will increasingly depend on whether the economy still possesses enough employment resilience to absorb another tightening step. signal: Jobs are now the counterweight to inflation.

Recursive System

Key takeaways

the Scarcity Premium

  • The winners of the next phase maximize Productive Intelligence ÷ (Capital + Energy + Memory + Bandwidth + Time).
  • Intelligence itself is becoming cheaper; the scarce complements underneath it are not.
  • The best AI exposure combines scarcity rents with proximity of cash flow — not distant, leveraged, refinancing-dependent cash flows.
  • For executives: design substitution across every scarce layer, not ownership of all of them.
  • For Europe: measure Productive Intelligence per Euro of Scarce Capital, not subsidy expenditure.
