The Week That Could Redefine the AI Trade.

Sintra, US payrolls, China PMI and Tesla may reveal where the next trillion dollars of market value will be created.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann June 29, 2026 4 min read Capital · Macro · Infrastructure
SintraECB Forum — shapes the policy debate
PayrollsUS jobs — defines the rate outlook
China PMItests the global industrial recovery
Tesladeliveries — the mobility transition

This week could mark an important turning point for financial markets. Not because of a single event — but because four critical catalysts will test the foundations of the AI Supercycle.

The ECB Forum in Sintra will shape the policy debate. The US employment report will define the interest-rate outlook. China’s PMI will test the strength of the global industrial recovery. And Tesla’s deliveries will provide another signal on the transition from automotive manufacturing to AI-enabled mobility.

Taken together, these events will help answer the question that increasingly matters for investors. Not whether AI will transform the global economy — it will. The real question is another one.

The question that matters

Where is the next trillion-dollar opportunity?

Our assessment is straightforward. The market is moving beyond the AI trade. The next winners are likely to be those controlling capital, accelerated computing, energy and physical infrastructure — not simply those building better models. The next phase will reward less the firm that develops the best model, and far more the one that can finance, power and deploy intelligence at industrial scale.

01

What’s next

Less euphoria, more discipline. Less narrative, more execution.

The next phase will be far more selective. Less enthusiasm. More discipline. Less narrative. More execution. The new leaders will be defined by four factors — and this is where the next competitive advantage will be built.

01

Capital

The advantage shifts to those who can fund intelligence at scale. Cheaper capital — not larger models — becomes the decisive variable as the AI race turns into a financing race.

02

Energy

Electricity is the binding constraint of the AI economy. Whoever secures abundant, affordable power controls the throughput of intelligence — and the margins that come with it.

03

Accelerated computing

Compute is the raw material of the supercycle. Access to it — at scale and at cost — separates the operators who can deploy AI broadly from those who merely experiment.

04

Deployment

Value accrues where intelligence meets the real economy. The winners will be measured not by what they build, but by how fast and how widely they put it to work.

Less euphoria, more discipline. Less narrative, more execution. This is where the next competitive advantage will be built — and where, in our view, the next trillion-dollar opportunity will emerge.
