10-year Yield Has Moved Above 5%, and Markets Are Assigning Around a 90% Probability to a Fed Hike This Week.

In an all-share consolidation battle, the institution that sustains the strongest equity currency long enough to make its architecture irreversible will win — not the one willing to pay the most.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 15, 2026 2 min read Capital

“Share currency is strategic power.”

Higher rates initially support banking margins. But they also raise funding costs, increase credit risk, compress valuation multiples and raise the hurdle rate for every acquisition.

The Risiko is therefore becoming simultaneously more strategic and more financially demanding.
