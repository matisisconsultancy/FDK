03

The Funding Equation

Who is competing for duration

  1. Lower oil can reduce inflation expectations. Slower employment can reduce the probability of additional monetary tightening. Neither eliminates the structural demand for capital.

    Signal Cyclical relief does not resolve a structural funding equation.

  2. Governments are financing deficits, defense, energy security and industrial policy. Hyperscalers are financing AI factories, power, data centres and networks. Utilities are financing grids. Companies are financing supply-chain resilience.

    Signal The cost of capital is no longer set by the central bank alone, but by the aggregate financing requirements of the Next Economy.

  3. The Treasury's decision not to increase long-term auction sizes provides tactical relief. It does not resolve the underlying funding equation. A structurally higher real rate may ultimately be one of the consequences of AI success—not evidence of AI failure.

    Signal Higher real rates may be a symptom of AI succeeding, not failing.

Hidden Patterns Beyond the News

  • Oil near $79 reflects diplomatic probability, not restored physical capacity.

  • Unchanged auction sizes offer tactical relief, not a structural fix.
  • AI is deflationary in labor but inflationary in capital formation.
