AI demand is not the problem — the economics of delivering it are, and with the 10-year Treasury testing 5%, capital must now prove the return.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann September 20, 2026 3 min read Capital

“The next winner will not have the most compute. It will have the highest return on compute.”

The AI race is no longer about building intelligence. It is about making intelligence pay. Models are improving and compute is expanding, but the scarce inputs are moving outward — from memory to power to grid to capital to materials to permission.

The U.S. 10-year Treasury breached 5% this week before retreating below it. So 5% is not today's fixed yield; it is the benchmark around which long-duration capital is being repriced. At the same time, Brent remains above $100, while Nscale reports more than $103B of contracted AI value and a power pipeline above 10 GW.

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

Who can scale AI and still earn a sustainable return above its full cost of capital?

The 5% Benchmark

02

Five-Signal Dashboard

evidence and verdict

  1. The U.S. 10Y breached 5%. Long-duration capital is being repriced around a higher regime. signal: Risky capital must earn more.

  2. Nscale reports over $103B contracted, a pipeline above 10 GW — and a $1.02B H1 loss. Demand is validated; returns are the test. signal: Demand is validated. Returns are the test.

  3. AI, critical minerals and trade are converging into a single contest. signal: The race is now full-stack.

  4. AI's potential productivity gain is roughly +1%, while data centers already consume about 3% of electricity. signal: The opportunity is below the model layer.

  5. Oil remains above $100. Secured power is now a source of edge. signal: Secure power is competitive advantage.

05

Investor Action — Three Steps

screen, own, avoid

1

Screen — Buy the Spread

Start with ROIC − WACC. If the spread is not sustainably positive, stop there.

2

Own — The Bottleneck + The Conversion

Favor assets combining scarcity, pricing power, secured energy, credible delivery, high utilization and strong balance sheets. Follow the conversion chain: backlog → capacity → utilization → cash flow → returns. For Europe, prioritize the physical stack.

3

Avoid — Capital Without Productivity

Penalize refinancing dependence, unsecured power, grid delays, customer concentration, low utilization and weak cash conversion. Capacity that cannot earn its cost of capital is not an AI asset — it is a capital liability.

Key takeaways

the hierarchy

  • Screen the spread. ROIC must sit sustainably above WACC.
  • Own the bottleneck. Scarcity, secured energy and pricing power.
  • Demand the conversion. Backlog must become cash flow and returns.
  • The supercycle is intact — but the cost-of-capital regime has changed.
