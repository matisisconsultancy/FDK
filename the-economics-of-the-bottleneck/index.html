The Economics of the Bottleneck

The AI profit pool is migrating away from intelligence itself toward the scarce layers that industrialize it — and the next phase rewards those who turn position into cash before capital intensity absorbs the advantage.

FdK Francesco de Leo Kaufmann October 2, 2026 8 min read Capital

“Velocity identifies the leader. The verdict identifies what can still break the thesis.”

The AI profit pool is migrating away from intelligence itself. As models proliferate, scarcity is moving along a chain — Memory → Packaging → Networking → Power → Chip Design → Security — while higher rates increase the cost of scaling every layer.

The emerging divide is Scarcity + Economic Capture versus Capital Intensity + Weak Conversion. Technology leadership remains essential, but the next phase will increasingly reward companies that can turn scarce positions into durable cash economics before the cost structure required to scale them absorbs the advantage.

The fundamental question: who can turn an indispensable position into durable economic value before capital intensity erodes the return?

03

The Two Decisive Signals

Where the economics are moving

  1. Model capability is proliferating faster than the harder-to-reproduce layers surrounding it. TSMC captures advanced manufacturing scarcity. Micron captures AI-memory scarcity. Schneider Electric captures power architecture. Synopsys monetizes design complexity. Palo Alto Networks benefits from the governance burden created by autonomous agents. signal: AI value is moving toward the infrastructure and control layers required to industrialize intelligence.

  2. AI is becoming more capital intensive precisely as long-duration capital remains expensive. Companies with strong cash generation can invest through volatility, absorb delays and maintain strategic flexibility. Companies carrying large fixed commitments face a harder conversion test. signal: Higher-for-longer changes competitive structure — not merely valuation.

Five Takeaways

The decisive divide

  • The economic center of AI is moving beyond models.
  • Resilience is becoming strategic power.
  • Funding provenance is becoming an investment variable.
  • Leaders fund expansion; laggards disproportionately fund repair.
  • The decisive divide is between strategic advantage already converting into cash and strategic advantage that still requires financial proof.
